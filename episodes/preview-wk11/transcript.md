## Cold Open

**Ref:** This is the Sixth Go Round, spoken edition, the Week Eleven Preview. To my left, Coach Dutch Boyle, who once drew up the seating chart for a team banquet and put the long snapper at the head table.

**Dutch:** He never had a bad snap again. That's coaching.

**Ref:** To my right, Nia Okafor, who has ranked every Thanksgiving side dish by how often it gets finished.

**Nia:** Stuffing is undefeated. And speaking of dinner, this league has a seating problem. Fourteen guests, ten chairs.

**Dutch:** Who's at the main table?

**Nia:** The top six seeds. They skip the wild-card week. Seats seven through ten are the kids' table, which means an extra game. And four guests are hoping for leftovers.

**Ref:** One name card is out so far. The author has his seat. Four more teams are one win away from theirs. Let's see who's sitting where.

## The Matchups

**Ref:** We start with the game of the week. The Robots at Win-telligence.

**Nia:** Simplest stakes on the board. Both teams have clinched a playoff berth, and the winner clinches a seat at the main table.

**Dutch:** Win-telligence have won five straight. Mahomes at home, Bucky Irving back from the bye.

**Nia:** And the Robots have Christian McCaffrey, coming off the best game anybody has had this season. This one could go all the way to Monday night. Daniels and Higgins for the Robots, Ja'Marr Chase for Win-telligence, all in the same game.

**Ref:** The author at the Badgers.

**Dutch:** The author has a chair. Now he wants the head of the table.

**Nia:** His chance at the top seed: ninety-three percent with a win, sixty-four percent with a loss. But the Badgers are playing for more. Joe Burrow has been on a tear, and Bhayshul Tuten has scored in four straight games.

**Dutch:** And they've got Burrow and Diggs on Monday night, opposite sidelines. Somebody on that roster is having a good night.

**Ref:** Chaos at Provid-End.

**Nia:** A win clinches a main-table seat for Chaos. They get D'Andre Swift back, and they need a kicker.

**Dutch:** Provid-End are sitting in the sixth chair. Last one at the main table. The byes took a pair of running backs and a receiver, but they've still got Josh Allen and Amon-Ra St. Brown, both at home.

**Nia:** Provid-End's chance at the top six: sixty-four percent with a win, twenty-one percent with a loss.

**Ref:** Green Eggs at Motorcity.

**Nia:** Motorcity are waiting on Kenneth Walker. He missed last week with a groin injury and didn't practice Wednesday.

**Dutch:** Green Eggs lose their quarterback and their defense to the same bye. That's a couple of empty plates. The wire can fill both.

**Ref:** Team 3 at Simmons. Closest game on the board.

**Nia:** And the most riding on it. Both teams just broke long skids. Team 3 have Purdy, Jefferson and Kittle, all in Mexico City on Sunday night.

**Dutch:** So they sit through the whole day with empty spots on the scoreboard. I'd need another helping of something.

**Nia:** Team 3's playoff chance: ninety-eight percent with a win, sixty-five percent with a loss.

**Ref:** Gofffather at Rickhouse.

**Dutch:** Rickhouse have the best lineup in the league on paper, and they start Thursday night with players on both sides. Stroud for Houston, Jonathan Taylor for the Colts.

**Nia:** Gofffather have won back-to-back games, and they were the underdog both times. A win clinches a main-table seat for Rickhouse.

**Ref:** And AMONsters at Infinity.

**Nia:** The byes flipped this one. Infinity are missing Bijan Robinson, Kyren Williams and Matthew Golden, and need a pair of running backs.

**Dutch:** And AMONsters walk in as the favorite. Henry, Hampton, and Carnell Tate back at practice.

## Who to Root For

**Ref:** New this week. Besides your own game, the one other result that helps you most. Nia?

**Nia:** The author wants Provid-End over Chaos. Rickhouse want the Badgers over the author.

**Dutch:** And who wants the author?

**Nia:** Four teams. Provid-End, Motorcity, Simmons and Gofffather are all pulling for the author this week.

**Dutch:** He's going to bring that up at dinner.

**Nia:** Infinity and the Badgers want Rickhouse. Team 3 want AMONsters. And Green Eggs and AMONsters both want Team 3.

**Ref:** And the Robots, Win-telligence and Chaos?

**Nia:** Nobody. Win, and the name card is yours.

## Directions to the Table

**Ref:** Nobody has been eliminated. So, directions for the four teams with the farthest to drive.

**Nia:** The Badgers have the short trip. Win two of their last three and they're very likely in.

**Dutch:** Gofffather have the fewest points in the league, so ties won't help. They need to pass four teams outright. The road goes through Motorcity and then the Badgers.

**Nia:** AMONsters and Green Eggs have the long road. Each has to win out, and three of the teams with four wins have to lose out.

**Dutch:** And they're both driving to the same folding chair.

**Nia:** Only one of them can take it. But both roads are open.

## Before Sunday

**Ref:** A lot of the NFL is off this week. Nia, who needs what?

**Nia:** Win-telligence and Chaos need a kicker: Drew Stevens and Joey Slye are out there. The Robots and Simmons need a tight end: Gunnar Helm or Greg Dulcich. AMONsters and Green Eggs need a quarterback: Cam Ward and Justin Herbert are both available.

**Dutch:** Justin Herbert, on the wire. What a league.

**Nia:** And Infinity need running backs. One of the options, Emari Demercado, plays Thursday night, so move early.
