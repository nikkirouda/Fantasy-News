## The Matchups

**Ref:** Road Islanders and Team Simmons. Islanders favored, comfortably.

**Nia:** The case for the Islanders is Jahmyr Gibbs, the biggest individual projection in the league. The tell is on the other side: Team Simmons have the first empty chair. Jonathon Brooks is on injured reserve and he is still in their running back slot.

**Dutch:** Kaelon Black and Samaje Perine are sitting on that bench staring at the phone. Somebody call them.

**Nia:** The concern for the Islanders is Aaron Jones. He sat out Wednesday's practice with the knee. And Simmons have a Questionable of their own in Zay Flowers.

**Dutch:** The game inside the game is Baltimore and Dallas, in Rio. Dak, Lamb and Flowers for Simmons. Javonte Williams and Pickens for the Islanders. Five starters on another continent.

**Nia:** Simmons won last week by half a point with a hole in the lineup. They know how to live dangerously. I would not assume they keep the chair empty.

**Ref:** Next. Captain Chaos and Rickhouse Royalty. Chaos favored, narrowly.

**Dutch:** Chaos are unbeaten after winning last week with three blanks in the lineup. That's either depth or a horseshoe. I have my suspicions.

**Nia:** The case for Chaos is Puka Nacua, who is Questionable, and Kyler Murray, who is cleared from the concussion protocol and starting in Tampa. The case for Rickhouse is Stroud, James Cook and Brock Bowers, who also carries a tag.

**Dutch:** And the trapdoor. Chaos start Jeremiyah Love at flex, and Rickhouse start the Niners defense against Love's Cardinals. Every Arizona carry is a little dispute of immovable D S T meets unstoppable R B.

**Nia:** It ends Monday night. D'Andre Swift for Chaos, behind a Chicago quarterback room that is likely down to Case Keenum, against DeVonta Smith for Rickhouse. Same field.

**Ref:** Next. Team Three and AMONsters Inc.

**Nia:** The household derby is over, and the loser drew the harder errand. AMONsters are the underdog by a lot.

**Dutch:** They have the nice kind of problem, though. Three quarterbacks. Burrow starts. Maye and Mayfield sit. And ESPN likes Maye better this week.

**Nia:** The game inside the game is Cincinnati at Pittsburgh. Burrow and the Bengals defense for AMONsters. DK Metcalf and Chris Boswell for Team Three. Whoever owns that one o'clock window probably owns the matchup.

**Dutch:** Team Three have Purdy and Jefferson. Saquon is Questionable after leaving hurt last week. You want Saquon on the field for that game against Chicago on Monday night. You don't want to find out Monday at eight.

**Nia:** And AMONsters start Darren Waller, the lowest projection of any tight end expected to play. I told you. It is always the tight end.

**Ref:** Next. Green Eggs and Sam and To Infinity and Bijan. Last week's high score against last week's low score.

**Nia:** The widest line of the week, and it is mostly one chair. Infinity's lineup still lists Caleb Williams, Doubtful with the hamstring. Daniel Jones is on their bench without a tag.

**Dutch:** Move the healthy quarterback into the quarterback slot. They teach that in Pop Warner.

**Dutch:** Green Eggs have Sunday night on both sides of the ball. Davante Adams and the Rams defense, against Waddle's Broncos, with Infinity's Kyren Williams in the same game.

**Nia:** A.J. Brown is on Green Eggs' injured reserve and Jaylen Warren is Questionable. They are favored anyway, by the width of an empty chair.

**Ref:** Next. Motorcity-Meh and Artificial Win-telligence. Win-telligence favored, barely.

**Nia:** Motorcity have scored more points than anybody in the league and still have a loss. This one is a lineup-management question before it is a football question. Dallas Goedert is Doubtful and still at tight end.

**Dutch:** With Mark Andrews and Pat Freiermuth on the bench. Two tight ends sitting behind an empty chair. That's a staffing issue.

**Dutch:** Collins is Questionable for Win-telligence, Evans for Motorcity. And Win-telligence start the Chargers defense in Buffalo. Lowest-projected defense in the league.

**Nia:** In Buffalo. In September. Against Josh Allen. That brings us neatly to the next one.

**Ref:** The Provid-End Zones and the Tritium Badgers. Closest projection of the week.
