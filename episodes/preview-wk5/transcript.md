## Cold Open

**Ref:** This is the Sixth Go Round, spoken edition, the Week Five Preview. To my left, Coach Dutch Boyle, who once taped an ankle during the national anthem and called it efficiency.

**Dutch:** The anthem is two minutes. An ankle is ninety seconds. You do the math.

**Ref:** To my right, Nia Okafor, who keeps a spreadsheet of every injury tag in this league, sorted by how worried she is.

**Nia:** Column F. Most of column F is Team 3.

**Ref:** Seven matchups. The first byes of the season, with Kansas City and Carolina off. And a Thursday night in Dallas that six of our lineups have a starter in. This week we open the Trainer's Room.

**Nia:** For every lineup, we count the starters carrying an injury tag this morning. Questionable, Doubtful, Out, all of it. A bye doesn't count. Travis Kelce isn't hurt, he's just not working.

**Dutch:** I've been in a lot of trainer's rooms. You learn more from who's on the table than from anything the coach tells you.

**Ref:** Tuesday we see whose tags turned into real absences, and who won carrying the most of them.

**Dutch:** And no prize for the healthy ones. A clean injury report is luck. I've had clean injury reports. I did nothing to earn them.

**Ref:** Nineteen tagged starters league-wide. One more thing this week. For every team we'll say what the best legal lineup from its own bench is worth, and where a free agent would help. We begin with the closest game.

## The Matchups

**Ref:** Robots Helping Themselves at To Infinity and Bijan. Two .500 teams, and Infinity favored by less than five.

**Nia:** And the Trainer's Room has a clear lean here. Infinity's lineup is spotless. Every injury on that roster is on the bench, including Caleb Williams, which is why Daniel Jones is the quarterback.

**Dutch:** The Robots have both receivers on the table. Malik Nabers and Tee Higgins, both Questionable. And they're starting Deshaun Watson while Jayden Daniels sits there projecting higher.

**Nia:** So the Robots' best quarterback decision is sitting on their bench. Daniels in, plus a kicker swap, is worth almost five points.

**Dutch:** And Infinity's best move isn't on their bench at all. Aaron Rodgers is a free agent and projects above Daniel Jones. That's three points sitting on the wire.

**Ref:** Infinity get a start tonight. Brandon Aubrey kicks in Dallas, and Kyren Williams closes their week Monday night in Los Angeles.

**Ref:** Motorcity-Meh at Team 3. Fix both lineups, and Team 3 is favored.

**Dutch:** I'll just say it. Team 3 is the busiest trainer's room in the league. Jefferson, Barkley, Skattebo, McConkey, four tagged starters. That's not a lineup, that's a waiting room.

**Nia:** And Barkley is projected for nothing this morning. The fix takes two moves, because DK Metcalf is a receiver and can't play running back. Braelon Allen slides from flex to running back, Metcalf comes in at receiver, and Isaiah Likely takes the flex. About fourteen points.

**Dutch:** Motorcity's room is quieter, but they've got two holes anyway. Kenneth Walker's a Chief, so he's on bye. And Lamar Jackson's tagged and projected for zero.

**Nia:** The answer is on their own bench. Trevor Lawrence at quarterback, Tyjae Spears for Walker, Dallas Goedert at tight end. That's nearly twenty-seven points, the biggest repair job in the league.

**Dutch:** And Tyler Allgeier's sitting on the wire projecting better than Spears. Even with all of it, Motorcity are still the underdog.

**Ref:** Tritium Badgers at Captain Chaos.

**Nia:** Chaos have the biggest bye-week bill in the league. Travis Kelce at tight end, Chuba Hubbard at running back, a Chief and a Panther, both in the lineup and both off this week.

**Dutch:** Kenyon Sadiq covers tight end. And for running back, Jeremiyah Love slides over from flex, and DJ Moore comes off the bench to take the flex.

**Nia:** Twenty-two points from their own bench, and Chaos go from underdog to favorite. The one free agent worth a look is a defense. The Jets project well above the Ravens.

**Ref:** Two more on the table for Chaos, Jeremiyah Love and D'Andre Swift. Puka Nacua and Chris Olave are carrying this team.

**Dutch:** Tritium have their own tags, Diggs and Kamara. But Jonathan Taylor in Pittsburgh and Trey McBride at tight end are why this one's only single digits.

**Ref:** Rickhouse ROYalty at AMONsters Inc. Rickhouse favored by about eighteen.

**Dutch:** I'll give AMONsters this: nobody in that lineup is tagged. Every one of their injuries is on the bench, Baker Mayfield included.

**Nia:** Rickhouse have one tag, on their best player. Brock Bowers is Questionable, in New England on Sunday.

**Dutch:** And the quarterback thing again. C.J. Stroud starts, Jacoby Brissett sits on the bench projecting higher. That's three and a half points. Somebody check on that.

**Nia:** Joe Burrow and Derrick Henry are the AMONsters' case, and the Cowboys defense plays tonight.

**Ref:** The Provid-End Zones at Green Eggs and Sam. Provid-End favored.

**Nia:** Green Eggs' lineup is healthy. Their injured players are just good ones. De'Von Achane and A.J. Brown, both on injured reserve.

**Dutch:** Healthy and thin. I've coached that team. You don't lose anybody on Sunday because you already lost them in September.

**Nia:** Matthew Stafford starts at quarterback this week, with Justin Herbert on the bench. Provid-End have two tagged starters, Rhamondre Stevenson and Jordan Addison.

**Dutch:** And Rashee Rice on their bench is tagged and on bye at the same time. That's a man with two excuses.

**Ref:** Josh Allen plays Monday night in Los Angeles, and Amon-Ra St. Brown plays Sunday afternoon in Arizona.

**Dutch:** Gofffather are winless, and they're starting a quarterback who isn't playing. Bryce Young is a Panther. Panthers are off.

## Trade of the Week

**Ref:** The trade of the week. AMONsters Inc. and Captain Chaos.

**Nia:** AMONsters have two starting quarterbacks, Joe Burrow and Drake Maye, and a weak spot at receiver. Chaos start Kyler Murray, and DJ Moore is on their bench. Burrow goes to Chaos, Moore goes to AMONsters.

**Dutch:** Both teams start the guy they get. AMONsters play Maye and put Moore in at receiver, and that's better than four points a week. Chaos get the better quarterback for the rest of the year.

**Nia:** About two points a week for Chaos, and the spare quarterback they've been carrying becomes a free roster spot. Moore's Questionable, so check the reports before anybody sends it.
