**Ref:** To my right, Nia Okafor, who built a playoff model on a napkin at a wedding and was asked to leave before the cake.

**Nia:** The model said they wouldn't last. Neither did the cake.

**Ref:** Week seven. The regular season is thirteen weeks long, so this is the exact middle. As many weeks behind us as ahead.

**Dutch:** Halftime. Oranges for everybody. Nobody touches the oranges until I say so.

**Nia:** And we're changing how we call the games. Every team is assumed to set its best lineup. Players back from bye go in, players tagged Questionable play unless the news says otherwise. We only talk about a hole if a team can't fill it without the waiver wire.

**Ref:** Three teams can't. We'll get to them. But first, the trade desk, because it's been a busy week for one man in particular.

## The Matchups

**Ref:** Robots Helping Themselves at Team Simmons. The Robots favored by about twelve.

**Nia:** Simmons have the first of our three holes. Their kicker, Drew Stevens, plays for Washington, and Washington is off. Simmons don't have another kicker on the roster. Ryan Fitzgerald of Carolina, or Chad Ryland of Arizona, is the fix.

**Dutch:** A kicker on the waiver wire. Every Sunday there's a kicker on the wire. Nobody ever says thank you.

**Ref:** The Robots had last week's high score, and Christian McCaffrey leads them again, with Malik Nabers and Drake London at receiver. Simmons finish Monday night, Dak Prescott and CeeDee Lamb in Dallas.

**Nia:** And here's what's at stake. A Robots win and they're nearly certain of the playoffs and probably skip the wild-card week. For Simmons this is the week the season tips. Win, and a top-six seat is more likely than not. Lose, and they're under .500, with one of the hardest schedules left in the league.

**Ref:** Motorcity-Meh at Rickhouse ROYalty. This is where the quarterback problem is.

**Dutch:** Lamar Jackson. Ankle. Missed two games, didn't practice Wednesday, and the reporting has him no better than even to play. And their other quarterback, Trevor Lawrence, plays for Jacksonville.

**Nia:** Also on bye. The welcome basket could go to Lawrence too.

**Ref:** If Jackson can't go, Motorcity need a pickup, and the obvious one is sitting there. Tyler Huntley, Jackson's own backup, has started Baltimore's last two games and is a free agent.

**Dutch:** Pick up the backup. It's the first thing they teach you. Then they teach you where the oranges are.

**Nia:** With a quarterback, it's Rickhouse by about five. Taylor makes his Rickhouse debut, and they lean on the Colts all day. Motorcity's two stars, Kenneth Walker and Jaxon Smith-Njigba, end up on opposite sides of the same Sunday-night game, Kansas City at Seattle.

**Ref:** And the stakes. The winner is nearly nine in ten to skip the wild-card week. The loser drops to about two in three and starts checking the mirrors. Motorcity have scored more points than anybody but the author. A losing record would waste them.

**Ref:** The Provid-End Zones at To Infinity and Bijan.

**Nia:** Infinity by more than three touchdowns. Last week they left more points on their bench than anybody in the league. This week Bijan Robinson and Kyren Williams are again the best pair of backs in the game, and both of them owe somebody a good day.

**Dutch:** Thursday night is the first quarter of this one. Infinity's Bears, Caleb Williams, Luther Burden and Colston Loveland, against Provid-End's Patriots, Rhamondre Stevenson and Hunter Henry. And Provid-End's kicker plays for Chicago. That's a lot of loyalty to sort out on a weeknight.

**Nia:** Provid-End have Amon-Ra St. Brown back from the Detroit bye, and Kirk Cousins at quarterback while Buffalo rests.

**Ref:** Stakes. Infinity are close to a must-win. A win keeps their playoff odds near four in five. A loss leaves them about even and sinking. For Provid-End, a win makes the playoffs very likely, and a loss puts them under .500 and hoping for help.

**Ref:** Tritium Badgers at AMONsters Inc. AMONsters favored by about ten.

**Dutch:** And here comes our traveler. The Badgers have a choice at quarterback: Aaron Rodgers in London first thing Sunday, or Joe Burrow against AMONsters.

**Nia:** They project within a fraction of a point of each other. If they start Burrow, he plays against the team that traded him ten days ago.

**Dutch:** Very awkward hallway. Bring the name tag.

**Ref:** The trade doesn't help the Badgers this week. Taylor is gone and Tuten is on bye, so it's J.K. Dobbins and Alvin Kamara, who's Questionable. AMONsters start Emanuel Wilson, the other half of the Burrow trade, next to Derrick Henry, and Drake Maye opens their week Thursday night.

**Nia:** Stakes. For the Badgers, a loss is close to fatal. A win keeps their playoff hopes around two in three; a loss cuts them roughly in half. AMONsters are already at the edge: a win keeps them a long shot, and a loss makes them a very long one.

**Ref:** Artificial Win-telligence at Gofffather. Win-telligence by about seventeen.

**Nia:** Win-telligence have four starters tagged Questionable: Jordan Love, Breece Hall, Bucky Irving and T.J. Hockenson. All four are expected to play. Ja'Marr Chase and Nico Collins are as good a pair of receivers as anyone's.

**Dutch:** Gofffather won last week with a man short. I respect that. I once won a game with ten men and a mascot.

**Ref:** Gofffather have Sam LaPorta back from the bye, and their week turns on two Questionable starters, Quinshon Judkins and Emeka Egbuka.

**Nia:** Stakes. Win-telligence have been at .500 for six weeks. A win makes the playoffs a strong bet. A loss puts them back on the line. Gofffather lose this one and it's about one in seven.

**Nia:** So they'll need whichever of Fitzgerald or Ryland Simmons doesn't take. And they lost Davante Adams to injured reserve this week, hamstring surgery, six to ten weeks.

**Nia:** This is the coin flip of the week. ESPN has Team 3 ahead by less than two points, and that's assuming two of their Questionable stars play: Justin Jefferson, back from the Vikings' bye, and Saquon Barkley, back from a hamstring that has cost him two games.
