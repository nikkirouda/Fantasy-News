## Cold Open

**Ref:** This is the Sixth Go Round, spoken edition, the Week Two Recap. All seven games are final. To my left, Coach Dutch Boyle, who once iced his own kicker.

**Dutch:** He needed the time to think. So did I.

**Ref:** To my right, Nia Okafor, holder of a doctorate in applied statistics, who has a model for the coin toss and would like you to know it is not fifty-fifty.

**Nia:** Fifty point eight. Heads. It matters more this week than usual.

**Ref:** It does. Three of seven games were decided by less than a point and a half. Small Change Sunday, for the purposes of this broadcast. The Anchor returns under the same rules: starter furthest below projection, kickers excluded, Nia holds the projections. We begin with the high score.

## Close

**Ref:** The Anchor. Nominations are closed. Nia holds the projections.

**Nia:** Seven nominated. The Anchor goes to Jaxson Dart of Captain Chaos: under a point against a projection of eighteen, the worst miss by any starter we tracked, and his team won the household derby anyway. Runner-up, Saquon Barkley of Team 3, three points on an afternoon that ended early, also on a winner. Third, Malik Nabers, Robots Helping Themselves.

**Dutch:** Two Anchors on winning teams. That's the week. The empties didn't decide Small Change Sunday. The second man did.

**Nia:** Checked, and he is right, which I say once a season. In all four games settled by under four points, the winner's second-best starter outscored the loser's second-best. Kelce behind Olave. Lamb behind Dak. St. Brown behind Allen. Kittle behind Purdy. That is the ruling. Retire the phrase; it has done its work. And for the other end of the measure: Smith-Njigba and Davante Adams, twenty-four over their forecasts each, one on a loser, one on a winner. The projections have been notified. They remain unmoved.

**Ref:** One thing to do before your next game: find your second-best starter. Not your star, the man behind him. This week's close games were not lost by the blanks; they were lost by the side whose second man came up short. A lineup with one star and eight passengers is a lineup waiting for Small Change Sunday. The ruling on the field stands.

**Dutch:** Go get 'em.
