## Cold Open

**Ref:** This is the Sixth Go Round, spoken edition, the Week Four Recap. All seven games are final, including Monday night in New Orleans. To my left, Coach Dutch Boyle, who once ran a two-minute drill with no timeouts, no headset and, by his account, no help.

**Dutch:** I had a whistle. A whistle is a timeout if you blow it loud enough.

**Ref:** To my right, Nia Okafor, who reads every injury report in the league before breakfast and has never once had to apologize for a lineup.

**Nia:** Once. It was a bye week. We do not discuss it.

**Ref:** This week, the story was the bench. Five of the seven losing teams had a winning lineup sitting on their own bench. So we have a new award, one week only: the Spare Key, for the team that left the win under the mat. The Anchor stays, same rules. And Thursday we promised to settle the Lions' Share. We begin with the high score.

**Dutch:** A key under the mat. In my day we had a guy who stood at the door.

**Nia:** That guy was the bench, Coach. Nobody let him in either.

## The Results

**Ref:** Team Simmons over Green Eggs and Sam. The high score of the week and the biggest margin.

**Nia:** Case is Tetairoa McMillan, Player of the Week, forty-five points for the Panthers on Sunday night. CeeDee Lamb was right behind him.

**Dutch:** And here's the part I like. Nobody on the Simmons bench would have added a point. A perfect lineup.

**Nia:** The only one in the league this week. No spare key, because every key was in the door.

**Dutch:** And Green Eggs?

**Nia:** A good week, honestly. Brian Robinson Junior went far past his projection, and they finally started the right quarterback, Stafford over Herbert, by a tenth of a point.

**Dutch:** A tenth of a point. I've seen bigger margins on a coin toss. Good week, wrong opponent.

**Ref:** Next. Road Islanders over The Provid-End Zones. The kitchen-table derby.

**Dutch:** The author beat his wife's team. I'd keep that quiet at dinner, but he's put it in the newsletter.

**Nia:** He is the only unbeaten team left. The Lions lost in Charlotte, but Goff, Gibbs and Jameson Williams all scored well anyway, and Javonte Williams did the heavy lifting from the flex.

**Dutch:** And the concern?

**Nia:** His best lineup was thirty points better than the one he set. And the defense on his bench outscored the defense he started.

**Dutch:** So the author has a spare key too. He just lives in a house with no lock.

**Nia:** For The Provid-End Zones, Josh Allen lost to New England, and the Patriots who beat him included Provid-End's own Rhamondre Stevenson and Hunter Henry. Then their Panthers defense finished in the red in a game the Panthers won.

**Dutch:** They rooted against themselves and lost both ways. That takes planning.

**Ref:** Next. Captain Chaos over Motorcity-Meh. The closest game of the week.

**Nia:** Case is Chuba Hubbard, running for Carolina on Sunday night. His night was worth more than ten times the final margin.

**Dutch:** And Puka Nacua. Started while ruled out on previous Sundays, and this week he actually played and led the team. Turns out it works better that way.

**Nia:** Motorcity's spare key was one move. Jaylin Noel, on the bench, in for Garrett Wilson, wins the game.

**Dutch:** One move. That's the one that keeps you up at night. Chaos sits second in the standings and Motorcity sits there doing the math.

**Ref:** Next. To Infinity and Bijan over Gofffather.

**Dutch:** Case is Kyren Williams. Nearly triple his projection.

**Nia:** And Infinity needed it, because Terry McLaurin was ruled out and started anyway. Gofffather had both sides of Sunday night, Bryce Young for Carolina and Sam LaPorta for Detroit, and both played well.

**Dutch:** So what happened?

**Nia:** Khalil Shakir sat on the bench while Courtland Sutton scored a point and change. That one swap wins the game.

**Dutch:** They're still looking for a win, and the win was on the bench, wearing a jersey, ready to go. Somebody hand Shakir the key.

**Ref:** Next. AMONsters Inc over Robots Helping Themselves.

**Nia:** AMONsters' first win of the season. Joe Burrow and Carnell Tate led it, and Jake Bates, their piece of the Lions' Share, kicked well.

**Dutch:** And Drake Maye outscored Burrow from the bench. But you win, nobody asks.

**Nia:** The Robots got big days from Tee Higgins and Malik Nabers. Their spare key took two moves, a running back and a kicker.

**Dutch:** Two moves is a key and a combination. I'll allow it.

**Ref:** Next. Artificial Win-telligence over Tritium Badgers.

**Nia:** Win-telligence started Nico Collins while he was out, twice. This week they started him while he was in, and he led the team.

**Dutch:** Progress. That's how you build a culture.

**Nia:** Tritium got a strong London morning from Jonathan Taylor, and Alvin Kamara, Thursday's trade-of-the-week piece, led them. But they flexed Dalton Kincaid for almost nothing, with Rome Odunze on the bench.

**Dutch:** And the quarterback call?

**Nia:** Darnold over Hurts. Wrong by a fifth of a point.

**Dutch:** A fifth of a point. Somewhere a coin is laughing.

**Ref:** Last. Rickhouse ROYalty over Team 3. The low score of the week.

**Dutch:** Concern is Team 3. Started Justin Jefferson while he was ruled out, with DK Metcalf on the bench.

**Nia:** And the bench could have won it, but only with three changes. Rickhouse made the quarterback call Thursday flagged, Stroud over Mariota, and it was right.

**Dutch:** And the strange part?

**Nia:** Rickhouse left more points on the bench than anyone in the league, and won going away.

**Dutch:** That's not a spare key. That's a key ring.

**Nia:** Green Eggs and Sam, with the longest playoff odds in the league.

**Ref:** After further review, the numbers from Week Four are confirmed as read.

## Close

**Ref:** The Spare Key. One week only. Nia holds the bench.

**Nia:** The Spare Key goes to Gofffather. Their best lineup, from players they already had, wins by about fourteen, and Khalil Shakir alone would have done it. It is the cruelest key of the week, because they are the one team without a win. Runner-up, Team 3, whose key needed three turns. The narrowest, Motorcity, one move from winning the closest game of the week.

**Dutch:** And the author?

**Nia:** His bench outscored every other bench in the league. He won anyway. The committee notes that winning is not the same as setting a good lineup.

**Dutch:** Retire the key. Put it back under the mat where nobody looks.

**Ref:** The Anchor. Nia holds the projections.

**Nia:** The Anchor goes to Rashee Rice of The Provid-End Zones, zero points against a projection of nearly fifteen. Runner-up, Ja'Marr Chase of Win-telligence. Third, Parker Washington of Rickhouse. Both runners-up won anyway. Not eligible, and noted: Justin Jefferson and Terry McLaurin, started while ruled out.

**Ref:** And the Lions' Share. Carolina won the game, and Carolina won the share. The Player of the Week and the swing in the closest game both came off the Panthers' sideline.

**Dutch:** Everybody bought Lions. The Panthers cashed the check.

**Ref:** One thing to do before your next game: look at your bench before you lock. Five losing teams had the win sitting right there this week. The ruling on the field stands.

**Dutch:** Go get 'em.
