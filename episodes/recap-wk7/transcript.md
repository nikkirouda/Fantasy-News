**Nia:** Fourteen teams, two branches each. I've had my finger on this button since Thursday, Ref.

**Nia:** The scoreboard is a model, Dutch. It's just a very short one.

## The Results

**Dutch:** And Team 3?

**Nia:** You'd have started whoever looked hungriest at the bus, Dutch.

**Dutch:** And I'd be right more often than you'd think.
