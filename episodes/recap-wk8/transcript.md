## Cold Open

**Ref:** This is the Sixth Go Round, spoken edition, the Week Eight Recap. To my left, Coach Dutch Boyle, who once signed a free-agent kicker off a parking-lot tryout and cut him before the bus left.

**Dutch:** He missed the bus. That was the tryout.

**Ref:** To my right, Nia Okafor, who wrote down on Thursday which three teams had to pick up a Jet, and has been checking the waiver wire every hour since.

**Nia:** Three teams, three holes, three Jets on the wire. I can tell you how many got claimed.

**Dutch:** Don't tell me yet. I want to enjoy the not knowing.

**Ref:** He'll get the answer in the second half. First, the scoreboard.

## The Results

**Ref:** The author over AMONsters, the biggest favorite of the week. It went to Monday night.

**Nia:** The author led by more than fifty on Sunday afternoon. Then Drake Maye threw a long touchdown to DeMario Douglas in Miami, and suddenly it was a twelve-point game with one AMONsters back still to play.

**Dutch:** Emanuel Wilson. Monday night. Seattle.

**Nia:** Seattle brought back two running backs that night. Wilson didn't record a carry or a catch. The lead held.

**Dutch:** The author's best player played with two broken ribs. Michael Wilson. Said so himself after the game.

**Ref:** Twenty-seven points with two broken ribs. The rest of the lineup was healthy and scored less.

**Dutch:** And?

**Nia:** Forty points. Best day by any player in the league. Player of the Week.

**Dutch:** Revenge is a dish best served with a passing touchdown.

**Nia:** Thursday also said Rickhouse's own defense would spend the day trying to stop Rickhouse's own running back. The defense won. Jacksonville's defense scored twenty-one for Rickhouse. Jonathan Taylor scored seven.

**Ref:** And Tony Pollard scored twenty-nine on the Rickhouse bench, behind two receivers who scored nothing between them.

**Ref:** Provid-End over Simmons. Simmons had gone loss, win, loss, win all season. This was supposed to be a win week.

**Nia:** It was a fifty-seven-point loss week. Tyler Allgeier didn't practice Wednesday, came off the bench into the lineup, and scored thirty-six on a projection of eight.

**Dutch:** Never trust a hamstring. Except that one.

**Ref:** Green Eggs over Team 3. Green Eggs end a five-game losing streak.

## Before Sunday

**Ref:** Nia. The three holes.

**Nia:** The Robots needed a defense. Team 3 needed a quarterback and a tight end. Gofffather needed a kicker. Thursday named the fixes, and the Jets could cover two of them.

**Dutch:** And how many got claimed?

**Nia:** None.

**Dutch:** None?

**Nia:** All three Jets sat on waivers and scored thirty-eight between them. Here's the part that saves everyone's dignity: none of those pickups would have changed a result. The Jets would have added twenty-three for Team 3, and they lost by twenty-six. Gofffather needed eighty-one.

**Ref:** And the Robots?

**Nia:** Played without a defense and won by less than five. They got away with it.

**Dutch:** That's not a strategy. That's a getaway car.
