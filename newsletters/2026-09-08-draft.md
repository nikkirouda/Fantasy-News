# Draft Recap & Way-Too-Early Projections

The offseason lies are over, the redraft dust has settled, and fourteen general managers now believe — however briefly — that this is finally their year. Below is your team-by-team breakdown, the way-too-early power rankings nobody asked for, and a strength-of-schedule chart that will absolutely be wrong by Week 4. Enjoy it while the record still reads 0-0 for everybody.

## The draft, team by team (projected starters)

### 1. Captain  Chaos — 2055.4 projected starter pts
Slot 5 GM opened with Puka Nacua and never looked back, stacking six wide receivers around a Kelce-Love-Swift core that projects as the best roster in the league on paper. The Omar Cooper flier at pick 220 (ADP 171) is the kind of late-round grave-robbing that wins leagues. The catch: this team's medical tent looks like a MASH unit, with Nacua, Love, Swift, Hubbard, and Sadiq all Questionable and Isiah Pacheco already on IR. Ceiling of a champion, floor of a walking injury report — buckle up.

### 2. Drafting the Leader — 2025.8 projected starter pts
McCaffrey at #9 headlines a WR-heavy build (six deep) anchored by Daniels under center, and the Ryan Flournoy dig at pick 216 is pure lottery-ticket value. Unfortunately CMC, Nabers, Higgins, and Keaton Mitchell all carry Questionable tags heading in, which is a lot of "we'll see" for a team ranked this high. Still projects second overall in raw talent — health is the only thing standing between this roster and a title.

### 3. Motorcity-meh — 1998.3 projected starter pts
JSN at #7 leads a balanced, three-tight-end-deep roster that quietly ranks third in projected points, with Pat Freiermuth (pick 218) as a nice bonus grab at tight end depth. Mike Evans, Cameron Dicker, and Jaylin Noel are all Questionable, and Jordyn Tyson is already on IR, so the name "Motorcity-meh" might end up being self-fulfilling if the injury bug keeps biting. Talented roster, shaky trainer's room.

### 4. Team Simmons — 1992.2 projected starter pts
CeeDee Lamb at #12 anchors a seven-wide-receiver monster of a roster, and Malachi Fields at pick 213 was pure value theft. Ashton Jeanty, Zay Flowers, and Jonathon Brooks are all Questionable, with Tank Dell already lost for the year on IR — so the RB room needs some things to break right. Fourth in projected strength with real ceiling if the ankle/knee reports clear up.

### 5. Road Islanders — 1976.9 projected starter pts
Yes, this is us. Gibbs at #1 overall gave the Islanders a true bell-cow anchor, and Evan McPherson at pick 224 was the definition of a steal — a top-tier kicker in the 22nd round is basically theft with a badge. The Jared Goff reach at #85 will either look like foresight or a punchline by Thanksgiving, and the health report (Tyler Warren, Jakobi Meyers, Kyle Monangai all Questionable) has this newsletter's author nervously refreshing the injury wire. Fifth in power, ninth-easiest schedule — no excuses if this doesn't work.

### 6. Artificial Win-telligence — 1967.0 projected starter pts
Chase at #3 gives this roster a true WR1, but good grief, the medical file: Chase, Mahomes, Hall, Henderson, and Kraft are all Questionable to start the year. The Deebo Samuel reach at pick 82 (ADP 144) raised eyebrows, though Malik Washington at 222 was a nice save. Sixth in projected strength — if even half the walking wounded suit up, this team is dangerous.

### 7. Tritium Badgers — 1956.7 projected starter pts
Jonathan Taylor at #4 headlines a run-first approach, with the Wan'Dale Robinson reach at pick 88 (ADP 112) drawing some side-eye and the Kayshon Boutte grab at 221 quietly offsetting it. Odunze, Wan'Dale, and Kamara are all nursing Questionable tags, which is a rough trio to have banged up in Week 1. Seventh overall, right in the mushy middle where this league lives.

### 8. To Infinity and Bijan! — 1949.4 projected starter pts
Bijan at #2 sets the tone for a well-rounded five-and-five RB/WR build, and Ty Johnson (pick 223) doubles as both the steal and a Questionable-tag concern. Luther Burden is also banged up entering the year, but this roster's depth across two tight ends and a clean QB situation with Caleb Williams keeps it comfortably mid-pack at #8.

### 9. Team 3 — 1944.3 projected starter pts
Justin Jefferson at #13 headlines a roster that somehow also found Chris Boswell as a 209th-pick steal — kicker war is heating up early. The bad news: DK Metcalf, George Kittle (Achilles surgery), and De'Zhaun Stribling are all Questionable, and Zach Charbonnet is on the PUP list recovering from ACL surgery. Ninth in projected strength, but the training room here is doing a lot of heavy lifting.

### 10. The Provid-End Zones — 1943.0 projected starter pts
Amon-Ra St. Brown at #6 anchors the only injury-free roster in the entire league right now — genuinely remarkable given everyone else's medical binder. The Hunter Henry reach at pick 107 (ADP 153) is a slight overpay, but Zachariah Branch at 219 balances the ledger. Tenth in power with a clean bill of health, which counts for a lot in November.

### 11. Green Eggs and Sam — 1913.3 projected starter pts
De'Von Achane at #11 headlines the RB room, but the real headline is drafting the Los Angeles Rams defense as a bona fide "steal" at pick 151 — analytics say bold, we say brave. Rachaad White and Tre' Harris carry Questionable tags entering the year. Eleventh in projected strength, with the widest range of outcomes of any team not named Chaos.

### 12. Gofffather — 1893.2 projected starter pts
Chase Brown at #8 leads a six-wide build that reached for Courtland Sutton at pick 49 (ADP 82), a decision this newsletter will be watching closely. Bryce Young at pick 217 was a nice value stash, but the injury report is rough: Egbuka, Pittman, LaPorta, Shakir, and Croskey-Merritt are all Questionable. Twelfth in power — depth will need to answer some early bells.

### 13. AMONsters Inc. — 1811.6 projected starter pts
Omarion Hampton at #14 leads a run-light, receiver-heavy (seven WRs!) build that also rostered three quarterbacks, for reasons. The Xavier Worthy reach at pick 98 (ADP 132) stands out, and Carnell Tate, Terrance Ferguson, and Jalen McMillan are all Questionable to open the season. Thirteenth in projected strength but gets the toughest schedule in the league (tied for #1 hardest) — a brutal combination.

### 14. Rickhouse ROYalty — 1806.1 projected starter pts
James Cook at #10 anchors a run-heavy roster that reached for Chris Godwin at pick 103 (ADP 124), though Jaylen Wright at 215 offset some of that cost. Bhayshul Tuten and Josh Downs are both Questionable, and this roster projects dead last in the power rankings while also drawing the league's hardest schedule. Rough combination to open a season with — but hey, someone has to be #14, and at least the name is great.
