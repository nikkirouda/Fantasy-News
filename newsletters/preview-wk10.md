# Week 10 Preview

Four weeks left in the regular season, and every team in this league still has something to play for. Nobody has clinched and nobody is out. The leaders are tied, a crowd is chasing the last top-six seats, and the teams at the bottom still have a path. This is the stretch where seasons turn, and this week has a game for everybody.

It starts Thursday night in New Jersey, with Washington at the Giants. On Sunday morning the Lions and Patriots play in Munich at 9:30 Eastern, so set those lineups before breakfast. Four teams are off: Philadelphia, Chicago, Tampa Bay and Denver. That's a big week for byes in this league, and six of our teams have a spot to fill. There's more on that at the bottom. Monday night, Jacoby Brissett makes his first start for Baltimore against the Chargers.

Same ground rules as always. Every team is assumed to set its best lineup, Questionable players play unless the news says otherwise, and where a team can't fill a spot from its own roster, the line includes the obvious free-agent pickup. The numbers are ESPN's week 10 projections as of late Wednesday night.

## The wire

No trades again. The NFL deadline came and went on Tuesday, and it reshuffled a few rosters here anyway. Keenan Allen is now a 49er, which makes Infinity's receiver a San Francisco receiver for Sunday's trip to Dallas. Jonathan Taylor stayed in Indianapolis, so Rickhouse's running back isn't going anywhere.

The waiver wire was busy. The author added Jordyn Tyson. Motorcity grabbed two receivers, Mack Hollins and Tutu Atwell. Infinity picked up Aaron Rodgers to cover Caleb Williams's bye.

### Motorcity-Meh (4-5) at Road Islanders (7-2)

The two highest-scoring teams in the league, head to head. The author has scored the most points; Motorcity are right behind. Last week's Player of the Week, Kenneth Walker, leads Motorcity in Atlanta, with Jaxon Smith-Njigba alongside him and Garrett Wilson expected back from his concussion. Their new pickup, Hollins, plays in Munich against the author's three Lions: Jared Goff, Jahmyr Gibbs and Jameson Williams. Goff was limited in practice Wednesday with a sore chest, then practiced fully on Thursday, so he looks set for Munich.

The author's late-afternoon question is George Pickens. His hamstring kept him limited again on Wednesday, and Dallas doesn't play until 4:25.

**Stakes.** The author's chance at the top seed: 74% with a win, 43% with a loss. A win also clinches a playoff berth. Motorcity's chance at the top six: 63% with a win, 25% with a loss.

### Team Simmons (3-6) at Captain Chaos (7-2)

The other leader faces a tricky week. D'Andre Swift and the Bears are off, and Puka Nacua is facing a multi-week absence, so Chaos have a running back spot to fill. Chuba Hubbard, Chris Olave and Travis Kelce are still a strong core. Jalen Coker steps into the flex.

Simmons have plenty to play for. CeeDee Lamb, who led the whole NFL in receiving yards last week, leads the way in Dallas, and Tetairoa McMillan and Zay Flowers give them depth. The Broncos defense is on bye, and Simmons have already grabbed the Commanders, who play tonight. One fun lineup call: Tyler Shough is projected just ahead of Dak Prescott at quarterback.

**Stakes.** Chaos's chance at the top seed: 32% with a win, 10% with a loss. A win clinches a playoff berth for Chaos too. Simmons's playoff chance: 80% with a win, 50% with a loss.

### Tritium Badgers (4-5) at Robots Helping (Themselves) (6-3)

The Robots are back at full strength, with Christian McCaffrey, Malik Nabers, Tee Higgins and Jayden Daniels all in the lineup. Daniels and Nabers face each other on Thursday night, so the Robots get a head start.

The Badgers have been climbing. Joe Burrow is coming off two huge weeks, and Trey McBride is one of the most dependable tight ends in the league. They need a kicker, and they'll want Kamara and Jakobi Meyers in the lineup.

**Stakes.** The Robots' chance at a top-two seed: 27% with a win, 9% with a loss. The Badgers' playoff chance: 97% with a win, 79% with a loss.

### The Provid-End Zones (5-4) at Artificial Win-telligence (6-3)

The two hottest teams in the league. Win-telligence have won four straight, and Provid-End are coming off last week's big upset. Josh Allen and Amon-Ra St. Brown lead Provid-End, and Christian Watson is coming off a huge afternoon. Their kicker, Cairo Santos, won Sunday night for Chicago last week and is off this week, and Provid-End have already picked up Jason Sanders to fill in.

Win-telligence get Patrick Mahomes in Atlanta and Ja'Marr Chase on Sunday night against Pittsburgh. Bucky Irving's Bucs are off, so Jordan Mason and Breece Hall carry the backfield. The Bears defense, last week's pick-six heroes, is on bye too.

**Stakes.** Win-telligence's chance at the top six: 92% with a win, 61% with a loss. Provid-End's: 75% with a win, 35% with a loss.

### Rickhouse ROYalty (6-3) at Green Eggs and Sam (2-7)

Rickhouse had the high score of the week last week and project as the best lineup in the league this week. C.J. Stroud, James Cook, Jonathan Taylor and Brock Bowers are a lot of firepower.

Green Eggs get Jaylen Warren back from Pittsburgh's bye, and Matthew Stafford leads a Rams offense that's suddenly short on receivers. With Warren back, their best lineup is a good one, and they're the kind of team that can surprise anyone on a given Sunday.

**Stakes.** Rickhouse's chance at a top-two seed: 41% with a win, 15% with a loss. Green Eggs' playoff chance: 3% with a win, under 1% with a loss.

### To Infinity and Bijan! (4-5) at Team 3 (4-5)

A big one for both. Team 3 have Brock Purdy and George Kittle against the Cowboys, Justin Jefferson at Green Bay, and Zach Charbonnet in Las Vegas. Infinity's Bijan Robinson plays Kansas City in Atlanta, Matthew Golden is coming off a huge day, and Aaron Rodgers takes the snaps for one week.

**Stakes.** Infinity's chance at the top six: 44% with a win, 13% with a loss. Team 3's playoff chance: 76% with a win, 39% with a loss.

### AMONsters Inc. (2-7) at Gofffather (3-6)

Both teams won last week, and both would love to make it two in a row. AMONsters have Drake Maye in Munich and Derrick Henry on Monday night against Omarion Hampton, who is also theirs. Gofffather have Bryce Young and Chase Brown, and need a receiver with the Bucs off.

**Stakes.** Gofffather's playoff chance: 16% with a win, 2% with a loss. AMONsters': 5% with a win, under 1% with a loss.

## Before Sunday

A friendly heads-up, with byes everywhere. Six teams started the week with a spot their own roster couldn't fill, and two have already taken care of it: Provid-End added a kicker and Simmons added a defense. For the other four, the free-agent pool has an answer:

- **Kicker:** the Badgers. Andre Szmyt is available.
- **Defense:** Win-telligence. The Colts defense is the best one left.
- **Running back:** Chaos. Audric Estime and Corey Kiner are both available.
- **Receiver:** Gofffather. Konata Mumpfield and Xavier Hutchinson are both available.

And one reminder for everybody: the Munich game locks at 9:30 Sunday morning.
