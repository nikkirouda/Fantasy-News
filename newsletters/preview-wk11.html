Week 11 Preview

Thanksgiving is a week away, and this league has a seating problem: fourteen guests and ten chairs. Six of those chairs are at the main table, where the top six seeds sit out the wild-card week. The other four are at the kids' table, which in this league means playing an extra game. And four guests are left hoping for leftovers.

So far one name card has been set out. The author has a seat at the main table. Four more teams are already in the dining room, each one win away from a main-table card. Everybody else is still circling, and nobody has been shown the door.

The football starts Thursday night in Houston, with the Colts at the Texans. A big slice of the NFL is off this week: Atlanta, Cleveland, Green Bay, the Rams, New England and Seattle. That leaves holes all over this league, and there's help for those at the bottom. Sunday night the Vikings and 49ers play in Mexico City, and Monday night it's Cincinnati at Washington.

Same ground rules as always. Every team is assumed to set its best lineup, Questionable players play unless the news says otherwise, and where a team can't fill a spot from its own roster, the line includes the obvious free-agent pickup. The numbers are ESPN's week 11 projections as of Wednesday evening.

The wire

No trades. The Badgers were the busiest team, adding a kicker in Cameron Dicker and a receiver in Elic Ayomanor. Team 3 picked up the Broncos defense, and the author added a rookie quarterback, Fernando Mendoza.

Robots Helping (Themselves) (7-3) at Artificial Win-telligence (7-3)

The game of the week, and the simplest one to explain: the winner gets a name card at the main table. Win-telligence have won five straight. Patrick Mahomes is at home against Arizona, Bucky Irving is back from his bye, and T.J. Hockenson is hoping to return from a wrist injury. The Robots answer with Christian McCaffrey, fresh off the best game anyone has had this season.

This one may not be settled until late. Both teams have starters in Mexico City on Sunday night, and both have more on Monday night: Jayden Daniels and Tee Higgins for the Robots, Ja'Marr Chase for Win-telligence, all in the same game.

Stakes. A win clinches a top-six seat for either team. The Robots' chance at a top-two seed: 44% with a win, 9% with a loss. Win-telligence's: 40% with a win, 6% with a loss.

Road Islanders (8-2) at Tritium Badgers (4-6)

The author has his chair and would like the one at the head of the table. Jahmyr Gibbs and Jared Goff are home against Tampa Bay, and Javonte Williams and George Pickens are home against Tennessee.

The Badgers are playing for a lot more. Joe Burrow has been on a tear, Bhayshul Tuten has scored in four straight games, and Trey McBride is as steady as tight ends come. Burrow and Stefon Diggs both play Monday night, on opposite sides, so the Badgers will have a say right up to the end.

Stakes. The author's chance at the top seed: 93% with a win, 64% with a loss. The Badgers' playoff chance: 94% with a win, 69% with a loss.

Captain Chaos (7-3) at The Provid-End Zones (5-5)

Chaos get D'Andre Swift back from his bye to join Chuba Hubbard, who has been playing like a star. They need a kicker this week, and the wire has one.

Provid-End hold the sixth seed today, the last chair at the main table, and the byes have hit them hard: a pair of running backs and a receiver are off. Josh Allen at home against Miami and Amon-Ra St. Brown at home against Tampa Bay are a fine place to start anyway.

Stakes. A win clinches a top-six seat for Chaos. Provid-End's chance at the top six: 64% with a win, 21% with a loss.

Green Eggs and Sam (2-8) at Motorcity-Meh (4-6)

Motorcity have scored the third-most points in the league and need the wins to match. The question is Kenneth Walker, who missed last week with a groin injury and did not practice Wednesday. Mike Evans is hoping to practice Thursday for the first time in a month. Trevor Lawrence and Garrett Wilson are good to go.

Green Eggs lose their quarterback and their defense to the Rams' bye, and the wire has an answer for both. Jaylen Warren leads the way in Philadelphia, and Jaylen Waddle was a full participant in practice on Wednesday after his concussion.

Stakes. Motorcity's playoff chance: 94% with a win, 77% with a loss.

Team 3 (5-5) at Team Simmons (4-6)

The closest game on the board, and the one with the most riding on it. Both teams broke long skids last week. Team 3 will wait all day for theirs: Brock Purdy, Justin Jefferson and George Kittle all play Sunday night in Mexico City.

Simmons get Dak Prescott and CeeDee Lamb at home against Tennessee, and Zay Flowers and Tetairoa McMillan in the same game in Charlotte, one on each sideline. They need a tight end for the week.

Stakes. Team 3's playoff chance: 98% with a win, 65% with a loss. Simmons's: 94% with a win, 58% with a loss.

Gofffather (4-6) at Rickhouse ROYalty (7-3)

Rickhouse project as the best lineup in the league, and they open on Thursday night with players on both sides: C.J. Stroud for Houston, Jonathan Taylor and their kicker for Indianapolis. Brock Bowers is day to day with an ankle injury; his coach says he doesn't need to practice to play.

Gofffather have won back-to-back games and get Bo Nix back from his bye, with Chase Brown on Monday night. They are the underdog here, and they have been the underdog in both of those wins too.

Stakes. A win clinches a top-six seat for Rickhouse. Gofffather's playoff chance: 37% with a win, 8% with a loss.

AMONsters Inc. (2-8) at To Infinity and Bijan! (4-6)

The byes have turned this one upside down. Infinity are without Bijan Robinson, Kyren Williams and Matthew Golden, and need a pair of running backs for the week. What they do have is a trio of Bears at home against New Orleans: Caleb Williams, Luther Burden and Colston Loveland.

AMONsters need a quarterback for the week, and with Derrick Henry, Omarion Hampton and Carnell Tate, who is back at practice, they are the favorite.

Stakes. Infinity's playoff chance: 89% with a win, 57% with a loss.

Directions to the table

Nobody has been eliminated, so here is the road for the four teams with the farthest to travel.

Tritium Badgers. The short drive. Win two of the last three, against the author, Green Eggs and Gofffather, and they are very likely in. The cleanest version has one of Motorcity, Infinity or Simmons finishing behind them.

Gofffather. They have the fewest points in the league, so a tie on wins won't help. They need to pass four teams outright. The route runs through Motorcity next week and the Badgers the week after: win both, and have each of those two drop one more.

AMONsters Inc. Win out, against Infinity, Simmons and Provid-End. Then three of the five teams with four wins have to lose all the way out, and Green Eggs have to drop a game.

Green Eggs and Sam. Win out, against Motorcity, the Badgers and the author. Then three of those four-win teams have to lose out here too, and AMONsters have to drop a game.

Those last two roads end at the same folding chair, so only one of them can take it. Both roads are still open.
