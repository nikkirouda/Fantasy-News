Week 12 Preview

This week the football never stops. It starts Wednesday night with the Packers at the Rams, then the Thanksgiving games, another on Friday afternoon, the usual Sunday, and Tampa Bay on Monday night. One fantasy week, spread across the whole holiday.

That makes this the week of the Thanksgiving clock: how much of each lineup is already finished by the time the pie comes out on Thursday night. Some teams will know their fate before Black Friday. One team will not have started eating.

Same ground rules as always. Every team is assumed to set its best lineup, and Questionable players play unless the news says otherwise. Nobody is on a bye, so nobody needs a pickup to field a team. The numbers are ESPN's projections for the week as of Wednesday morning.

The wire

No trades, and the deadline is next Wednesday at noon. Two waiver claims went through early Wednesday: the author added a tight end, Tyler Higbee, and Green Eggs added Dylan Sampson, who goes straight into their lineup.

Road Islanders (9-2) at Robots Helping (Themselves) (7-4)

The clock's two extremes, in the same game. The author's Lions and Cowboys play on Thanksgiving, and so does his kicker in Buffalo, so by Thursday night he will have a score on the board and nothing left to do but watch. The Robots will not have played a down. Their first starter, Jonah Coleman, kicks off on Friday afternoon, and the rest of their lineup waits for Sunday: Jayden Daniels, Christian McCaffrey, Malik Nabers, and Atlanta's Kyle Pitts and Drake London in Minnesota.

So the Robots get to play the second half knowing exactly what they need. They are the favorite on paper anyway.

Stakes. A win clinches a top-six seat for the Robots. The author's chance at the top seed: >99% with a win, 86% with a loss. The Robots' chance at a top-two seed: 18% with a win, <1% with a loss.

Artificial Win-telligence (8-3) at Captain Chaos (8-3)

Two teams with their byes in hand, playing for the second seed. Win-telligence are the league's hottest team. Patrick Mahomes opens their week on Thanksgiving night in Buffalo, then Ja'Marr Chase, Nico Collins and Breece Hall on Sunday, and Bucky Irving last of all on Monday night.

Chaos have one of the busier Thursdays: D'Andre Swift in Detroit, then Travis Kelce and DJ Moore on opposite sides in Buffalo. Chuba Hubbard closes their week on Monday night.

Stakes. Win-telligence's chance at a top-two seed: 64% with a win, 10% with a loss. Chaos's: 44% with a win, 2% with a loss.

Tritium Badgers (4-7) at Green Eggs and Sam (2-9)

The Badgers need this one, and nearly all of their week happens on Sunday: Joe Burrow at home against New Orleans, Trey McBride in Arizona, and Bhayshul Tuten, listed as questionable, at home against Tennessee.

Green Eggs are out of the race and free to enjoy it. Their week opens Wednesday night with the Rams defense, and their new back, Sampson, plays on Sunday night.

Stakes. The Badgers' playoff chance: 84% with a win, 50% with a loss. A loss also ends their shot at the top six.

The Provid-End Zones (5-6) at Team 3 (5-6)

The game of the week in the middle of the table: same record, and the winner is all but in. Provid-End are near the top of the clock, with Christian Watson on Wednesday night, then Amon-Ra St. Brown, Josh Allen and Rashee Rice on Thanksgiving.

Team 3 wait. Saquon Barkley plays in Dallas on Thursday, but Brock Purdy and George Kittle are home against Seattle on Sunday afternoon, and Justin Jefferson is home against Atlanta. Denzel Boston, a questionable receiver, is the one to watch.

Stakes. Provid-End's playoff chance: >99% with a win, 79% with a loss. Team 3's: 98% with a win, 58% with a loss.

Motorcity-Meh (5-6) at Gofffather (4-7)

Motorcity's best lineup is one of the strongest in the league, and its biggest question plays on Thanksgiving night: Kenneth Walker, back at practice on a limited basis Tuesday and listed as questionable. Jaxon Smith-Njigba and Mike Evans meet in San Francisco on Sunday, one on each sideline.

Gofffather need the win to stay in the hunt. Sam LaPorta and the Cowboys defense get them started on Thursday, and Bryce Young finishes it on Monday night.

Stakes. Motorcity's playoff chance: >99% with a win, 87% with a loss. Gofffather's: 30% with a win, 2% with a loss.

Team Simmons (5-6) at AMONsters Inc. (3-8)

Simmons hold the sixth seat, the last one with a bye, and they front-load: Dak Prescott, CeeDee Lamb and their kicker all play on Thanksgiving. Ashton Jeanty and Harold Fannin close out Sunday night in Cleveland.

AMONsters are still alive, barely, and this is the week that settles it. Justin Herbert is listed as questionable for Sunday against New England.

Stakes. Simmons's chance at the top six: 51% with a win, 13% with a loss. AMONsters' playoff chance: 1% with a win; a loss eliminates them.

Rickhouse ROYalty (8-3) at To Infinity and Bijan! (4-7)

Infinity are second on the clock: Kyren Williams and Matthew Golden on Wednesday night, on opposite sides, then the Bears' Caleb Williams, Luther Burden and Colston Loveland in Detroit on Thanksgiving. Bijan Robinson finishes their week on Sunday.

Rickhouse spend most of their week on Sunday, starting with C.J. Stroud at home against Baltimore. Brock Bowers is listed as questionable for the Sunday night game in Cleveland.

Stakes. Rickhouse's chance at a top-two seed: 45% with a win, 10% with a loss. Infinity's playoff chance: 82% with a win, 35% with a loss.
