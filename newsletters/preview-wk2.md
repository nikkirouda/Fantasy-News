# Week 2 Preview

Welcome to Thursday. Seven matchups, one married couple on opposite sidelines, and a single NFL game, Dallas at Washington on Sunday afternoon, with starters in three different fantasy matchups riding on it. The projections below are ESPN's as of Thursday morning and will move by kickoff.

## This week's matchups

### Road Islanders (1-0) vs To Infinity and Bijan! (0-1)

- Projected: Road Islanders 125.9 — To Infinity and Bijan! 121.7 · favorite Road Islanders by 4.2 · win probability 52–48

Your correspondent draws the team that produced the Player of the Week in Week 1, and he is back: Caleb Williams carries the bigger quarterback projection in this game, and Colston Loveland, last week's zero at tight end, is pencilled in for eleven and change as if nothing happened. The Road Islanders' case is Jahmyr Gibbs, the highest-projected player in the entire league this week, and he plays Thursday night, so the author will spend Friday through Sunday either defending a lead or explaining one. Two real games decide this one from opposite sides: George Pickens and Javonte Williams for the Islanders against Terry McLaurin and the Bijan kicker in Dallas–Washington, and Aaron Jones running at the Bears defense while three Bears starters try to score on his Vikings. A note from the recap: the author has kept faith with Pickens. The projection agrees; the committee is reserving judgment.

### Team 3 (0-1) vs Robots Helping (Themselves) (1-0)

- Projected: Team 3 118.0 — Robots Helping (Themselves) 120.3 · favorite Robots Helping (Themselves) by 2.3 · win probability 51–49

The closest projection of the week, and the strangest shape: the two sides have split the San Francisco offense between them. Team 3 has Brock Purdy and George Kittle, Robots have Christian McCaffrey and the 49ers' kicker, so every Niners touchdown against Miami on Sunday afternoon is good news for exactly one of them, depending on who scores it. Team 3's case is Justin Jefferson, their biggest projection again after last week's star turn; Robots ride McCaffrey, one of the five largest projections in the league. Team 3 carries two Questionable tags, Ladd McConkey and Kittle, and Robots carry none among their skill starters. The Texans defense, minus four last week, is back in the Robots lineup at home to Cincinnati. And nobody should plan an early night: Cam Skattebo for Team 3 and Blake Corum and Malik Nabers for Robots all play Monday night in Los Angeles, so the closest game of the week is also the last one settled.

### Green Eggs and Sam (0-1) vs Gofffather (0-1)

- Projected: Green Eggs and Sam 107.1 — Gofffather 111.9 · favorite Gofffather by 4.8 · win probability 52–48

The only matchup between two teams that lost, so somebody leaves the weekend at 0-2, and the model can barely separate them. The game inside the game is Denver–Jacksonville at 4:05: Gofffather starts Bo Nix and Courtland Sutton for the Broncos, Green Eggs and Sam answer with Jaylen Waddle for Denver and Brian Thomas Jr. plus the Jacksonville kicker across the field, five starters in one stadium. Green Eggs and Sam's best argument is Justin Herbert, the largest projection on either side, and De'Von Achane at San Francisco. Gofffather's roster quietly hedges itself, with Quinshon Judkins for Cleveland and Emeka Egbuka for Tampa Bay in the same game, and waits until Thursday night's first snap for Sam LaPorta. Thomas Jr. and Michael Pittman Jr. both carry Questionable tags, one per side, and Green Eggs and Sam are not done until Monday night, when Davante Adams and the Rams defense take the field together.

### Captain Chaos (1-0) vs AMONsters Inc. (0-1)

- Projected: Captain Chaos 122.4 — AMONsters Inc. 103.2 · favorite Captain Chaos by 19.2 · win probability 60–40

The household derby. Captain Chaos and AMONsters Inc. are married, which makes this the only matchup in the league where the loser hears about it at breakfast. The model gives Chaos the comfortable side of it, and the lineups explain why: Puka Nacua carries the largest wide receiver projection in the league, with Chris Olave, Travis Kelce and a deep flex behind him, while AMONsters lean on Derrick Henry, twenty over his number last week, and Omarion Hampton, with Marvin Harrison Jr. and Mike Gesicki projected among the lowest starters at their positions anywhere in the league. The first argument starts at one o'clock, when Olave's Saints visit Henry's Ravens. The last one starts late: AMONsters finish on Sunday afternoon, but Chaos wait on Monday night for both Nacua and Jaxson Dart, on opposite sides of Giants–Rams.

### Artificial Win-telligence (1-0) vs Rickhouse ROYalty (0-1)

- Projected: Artificial Win-telligence 121.7 — Rickhouse ROYalty 97.3 · favorite Artificial Win-telligence by 24.4 · win probability 63–37

The widest projected gap of the week, and the model is only repeating what the lineups say. Win-telligence has Ja'Marr Chase and Nico Collins facing each other in Houston, so one of their own receivers will spoil the other's afternoon and the total will not care, plus Patrick Mahomes on Sunday night against the Colts. Rickhouse ROYalty start Malik Willis with the lowest quarterback projection in the league, Michael Mayer with the lowest tight end projection, and a Jaguars defense projected under five. Three of the softest numbers in the league on one side of one game is how a line gets this lopsided. Rickhouse's best hope of an early lead is James Cook on Thursday night; Collins is the Questionable tag to watch on the other side.

### Tritium Badgers (1-0) vs Team Simmons (0-1)

- Projected: Tritium Badgers 112.0 — Team Simmons 119.9 · favorite Team Simmons by 7.9 · win probability 53–47

The one matchup where last week's winner is this week's underdog: the unbeaten Badgers are projected behind a Team Simmons side that lost by the biggest margin of Week 1 and still scored more than half the league. Simmons's Dallas pair, Dak Prescott and CeeDee Lamb, play Washington at 4:25 while the Badgers' Stefon Diggs plays for Washington in the same game, the third fantasy matchup this week hanging on that one afternoon in Arlington. The Badgers' edge is Trey McBride, the highest tight end projection in the league, and Jonathan Taylor waiting for Sunday night at Kansas City. Simmons's soft spot is Jaydon Brooks, the lowest-projected running back starter anywhere in the league; their swing piece is Zay Flowers at the flex, the biggest flex projection on the board and a Questionable tag. If Flowers sits, this line moves.

### The Provid-End Zones (1-0) vs Motorcity-Meh (1-0)

- Projected: The Provid-End Zones 118.6 — Motorcity-Meh 128.6 · favorite Motorcity-Meh by 10.0 · win probability 54–46

The only game between two winners, and Motorcity-Meh bring the highest projection in the league into it: Lamar Jackson, Kenneth Walker III after his twenty-over Week 1, and Jaxon Smith-Njigba in Arizona. The Provid-End Zones open Thursday night with a lineup at war with itself: Josh Allen throwing for Buffalo, Amon-Ra St. Brown catching for Detroit, and the Lions defense trying to stop Allen, projected under four, the lowest defense number in the league. By Friday morning the Provid-End Zones will know a third of their score and Motorcity will know none of theirs; Walker does not play until Sunday night. Whoever wins here is the first team in the league to 2-0 with a scalp to show for it.
