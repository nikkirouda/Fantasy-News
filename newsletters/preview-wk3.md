# Week 3 Preview

Welcome to Thursday. Seven matchups, no byes, one NFL game played in Rio de Janeiro, and a new piece of furniture in the league: the empty chair. Four lineups this morning start somebody the injury report does not expect in the building. The projections are ESPN's as of Thursday morning and will move by kickoff, and every chair can still be filled before its game locks.

## This week's matchups

### Road Islanders (2-0) vs Team Simmons (1-1)

- Projected: Road Islanders 121.8 — Team Simmons 105.0 · favorite Road Islanders by 16.8

Your correspondent's team is unbeaten and carries the biggest individual projection in the league in Jahmyr Gibbs, which is exactly the kind of sentence that gets written right before something goes wrong. Team Simmons have the first empty chair: Jonathon Brooks is on injured reserve and still in their running back slot, with Kaelon Black and Samaje Perine on the bench waiting for the phone to ring. The game inside the game is Baltimore against Dallas in Rio, where Dak Prescott, CeeDee Lamb and Zay Flowers line up for Simmons and Javonte Williams and George Pickens for the Islanders, five starters on another continent. Two tags to watch: Flowers is Questionable, and the Islanders' Aaron Jones sat out Wednesday's practice with the knee. Simmons won last week by half a point with a hole in the lineup, so nobody on the favored side is relaxing.

### Captain Chaos (2-0) vs Rickhouse ROYalty (1-1)

- Projected: Captain Chaos 121.5 — Rickhouse ROYalty 116.8 · favorite Captain Chaos by 4.7

Captain Chaos are unbeaten after winning last week with three blanks in the lineup, which means they are either very deep or very lucky, and the league would like to know which. Their headliner, Puka Nacua, is Questionable; their quarterback, Kyler Murray, is cleared from the concussion protocol and starting in Tampa. Rickhouse answer with C.J. Stroud, James Cook and Brock Bowers, who carries a Questionable tag of his own. The trapdoor is in Santa Clara: Chaos start Jeremiyah Love at flex and Rickhouse start the 49ers defense against Love's Cardinals, so every Arizona carry is a small argument between these two lineups. Monday night settles it, with D'Andre Swift for Chaos, behind a Chicago quarterback room likely down to Case Keenum, against DeVonta Smith for Rickhouse in the same game.

### Team 3 (1-1) vs AMONsters Inc. (0-2)

- Projected: Team 3 121.9 — AMONsters Inc. 101.8 · favorite Team 3 by 20.1

The household derby is over and the loser drew the harder errand. AMONsters have the pleasant kind of quarterback question, three of them: Joe Burrow starts, Drake Maye and Baker Mayfield sit, and ESPN projects Maye above Burrow this week. The game inside the game is Cincinnati at Pittsburgh, with Burrow and the Bengals defense for AMONsters against DK Metcalf and Chris Boswell for Team 3, so whoever owns that one o'clock window probably owns the matchup. Team 3 lead with Brock Purdy and Justin Jefferson, and Saquon Barkley is Questionable after leaving last week hurt. AMONsters start Darren Waller, the lowest projection of any tight end expected to play.

### Green Eggs and Sam (1-1) vs To Infinity and Bijan! (0-2)

- Projected: Green Eggs and Sam 111.8 — To Infinity and Bijan! 91.4 · favorite Green Eggs and Sam by 20.4

Last week's high score against last week's low score, and the widest line of the week is mostly one chair. Infinity's lineup still lists Caleb Williams, Doubtful with the hamstring, while Daniel Jones sits on their bench without a tag; Chicago is likely down to Case Keenum on Monday night, which also bears on Colston Loveland and Luther Burden. Infinity at least start early: Bijan Robinson and Matthew Golden play tonight in Green Bay, so they will know a slice of their score before most of the league has set a lineup. Green Eggs have Sunday night on both sides of the ball, Davante Adams and the Rams defense against Jaylen Waddle's Broncos, with Infinity's Kyren Williams in the same game. A.J. Brown is on Green Eggs' injured reserve and Jaylen Warren is Questionable.

### Motorcity-Meh (1-1) vs Artificial Win-telligence (1-1)

- Projected: Artificial Win-telligence 120.7 — Motorcity-Meh 119.1 · favorite Artificial Win-telligence by 1.6

Motorcity have scored more points than anybody in the league and have a loss to show for it anyway. This line is a lineup-management question before it is a football one: Dallas Goedert is Doubtful and still at tight end, with Mark Andrews and Pat Freiermuth on the bench, and whichever of them moves in tilts the whole thing. Oddly, both sides want the same game to go well, since Win-telligence start Patrick Mahomes and Motorcity start Kenneth Walker for the Chiefs in Miami. Nico Collins is Questionable for Win-telligence and Mike Evans for Motorcity. Win-telligence's soft spot is the Chargers defense at Buffalo, the lowest-projected defense in the league.

### The Provid-End Zones (2-0) vs Tritium Badgers (1-1)

- Projected: Tritium Badgers 116.0 — The Provid-End Zones 115.0 · favorite Tritium Badgers by 1.0

The closest projection of the week, and the one coin flip this edition will spend. Provid-End are unbeaten on the arm of Josh Allen, the top quarterback projection in the league, at home to the Chargers; Tritium lost last week by half a point and a missed field goal. The angle here is the clock. Provid-End finish on Sunday except for their kicker, Cairo Santos, who kicks for Chicago on Monday night against Tritium's Jalen Hurts, with Tritium's Rome Odunze on the Bears side of the same field. Travis Etienne is Questionable for Tritium, and Trey McBride, the top tight end projection in the league, is their steady hand.

### Gofffather (0-2) vs Robots Helping (Themselves) (1-1)

- Projected: Gofffather 106.8 — Robots Helping (Themselves) 98.2 · favorite Gofffather by 8.6

The empty chair with the most riding on it. Jayden Daniels is Out, he is the Robots' starting quarterback, and there is no other quarterback on their roster, though there are two kickers. The waiver wire is the Robots' assignment before Sunday. Winless Gofffather have the opposite problem: Bryce Young on their bench projects a hair above Bo Nix, who starts. Robots open tonight with Drake London and Kyle Pitts in Green Bay, Christian McCaffrey is their biggest number, and Malik Nabers is Questionable. Nix and Courtland Sutton wait for Sunday night in Denver, across the field from the Robots' Blake Corum, so this one ends late.

## Before Sunday

Four empty chairs this morning, belonging to Simmons, Infinity, Motorcity and the Robots, and every one of them can be filled before its game locks. Atlanta at Green Bay tonight carries starters for five teams, and Sunday afternoon's game in Rio carries starters for eight. No byes this week, so there is no excuse on the schedule.
