# Week 4 Preview

Welcome to Thursday. Seven matchups, no byes again, one game in London at breakfast time, and a Sunday night in Charlotte that five of this week's seven matchups have money on. The headline act there is Detroit, so this edition keeps a small ledger called the Lions' Share: who owns a piece of the Lions, and who has to watch them play the Panthers before knowing the result. The projections are ESPN's as of Wednesday night and will move by kickoff.

## This week's matchups

### Road Islanders (3-0) vs The Provid-End Zones (2-1)

- Projected: Road Islanders 127.5 — The Provid-End Zones 113.2 · favorite Road Islanders by 14.3

The kitchen-table derby: your correspondent's team, still the league's only unbeaten, against his wife's. This is where the Lions' Share starts and very nearly ends, since Jared Goff, Jahmyr Gibbs and Jameson Williams all play Sunday night in Charlotte. The Provid-End Zones have an answer waiting in the same building: Amon-Ra St. Brown, a Lion of their own, and the Panthers defense, which spends the evening trying to stop the Islanders' entire Detroit wing. Before that, Josh Allen, the top quarterback projection of the week, hosts New England, and Provid-End's Rhamondre Stevenson and Hunter Henry play for the visitors, so their quarterback spends the afternoon playing against two of their own starters. Jadarian Price is Questionable, and Jakobi Meyers carries a tag on the Islanders' bench.

### Captain Chaos (2-1) vs Motorcity-Meh (2-1)

- Projected: Captain Chaos 133.3 — Motorcity-Meh 127.1 · favorite Captain Chaos by 6.2

The two biggest projections in the league are facing each other, and the team that has scored the most points all season is the underdog. Chaos lead with Puka Nacua, Questionable again, and Kyler Murray in Minnesota. Motorcity answer with Jaxon Smith-Njigba and Lamar Jackson, who also carries a Questionable tag, as does Mike Evans at flex. Chaos hold their piece of Sunday night from the Carolina sideline, where Chuba Hubbard carries against the Lions. Motorcity's tight end question from last week is settled, with Mark Andrews in and Dallas Goedert, now Doubtful, on the bench.

### Team 3 (2-1) vs Rickhouse ROYalty (2-1)

- Projected: Rickhouse ROYalty 122.6 — Team 3 118.0 · favorite Rickhouse ROYalty by 4.6

Team 3 get the first word tonight in Cleveland, where they start DK Metcalf and Chris Boswell for Pittsburgh and the Browns defense against them, so whatever happens Thursday, part of Team 3 is unhappy about it. The bigger worry is that both starting receivers, Justin Jefferson and Ladd McConkey, are Questionable, with Denzel Boston the receiver waiting on the bench. Rickhouse came from last in every column after Week 1 to a winning record on the back of Brock Bowers, who plays in Las Vegas against Kansas City. Their one decision is at quarterback: Marcus Mariota, on the bench, projects higher than C.J. Stroud, the starter. DeVonta Smith is Questionable.

### Green Eggs and Sam (1-2) vs Team Simmons (1-2)

- Projected: Team Simmons 111.6 — Green Eggs and Sam 87.1 · favorite Team Simmons by 24.5

The widest line of the week, and most of it is one roster slot. De'Von Achane, last week's Anchor, is on injured reserve and still in Green Eggs' running back slot, projected for nothing. Green Eggs also start Justin Herbert at quarterback, while Matthew Stafford, the bench quarterback who beat Herbert last week, again projects higher. Team Simmons have scored more points than all but four teams and have a losing record to show for it. Their quarterback question goes the same way as Green Eggs': Tyler Shough on the bench projects above Dak Prescott, who plays Houston alongside CeeDee Lamb. Tetairoa McMillan gives Simmons a share of Sunday night as a Panther, Zay Flowers is Questionable, and Harold Fannin Jr. plays tonight in Cleveland.

### Robots Helping (Themselves) (2-1) vs AMONsters Inc. (0-3)

- Projected: Robots Helping (Themselves) 108.5 — AMONsters Inc. 99.8 · favorite Robots by 8.7

The Robots found a quarterback, kept him, and open tonight: Deshaun Watson starts in Cleveland against Pittsburgh, with Jayden Daniels, now Questionable, on the bench. They also close the week, since Drake London and Kyle Pitts play in New Orleans on Monday night. Their record is better than their roster, and the Robots will happily take it. AMONsters are winless with the hardest schedule left in the league, and their quarterback room is still crowded: Joe Burrow starts, and Drake Maye on the bench projects about as high. AMONsters' Lions' Share is a kicker, Jake Bates, plus Darren Waller catching passes for Carolina on the other side of the same game. Derrick Henry is their biggest number, at home to Tennessee.

### Artificial Win-telligence (1-2) vs Tritium Badgers (2-1)

- Projected: Tritium Badgers 115.6 — Artificial Win-telligence 102.9 · favorite Tritium Badgers by 12.7

Win-telligence started Nico Collins while he was out two weeks running, and this week the question is Breece Hall: Doubtful and still in the running back slot, with TreVeyon Henderson on the bench. Collins and Bucky Irving are both Questionable. Tritium are up early, with Jonathan Taylor and Stefon Diggs in London at 9:30 Sunday morning; Tritium will have a score before most of the league has had breakfast. Trey McBride is Tritium's steady hand at tight end. Travis Etienne is Out and on their bench, and Alvin Kamara plays Monday night.

### Gofffather (0-3) vs To Infinity and Bijan! (1-2)

- Projected: To Infinity and Bijan! 114.9 — Gofffather 109.2 · favorite Infinity by 5.7

Gofffather are winless, and a single-digit line is a door left open. Bryce Young is their starting quarterback again, the bench quarterback Bo Nix outscored him last week, and this time Young projects higher. Young plays Sunday night at home against Detroit, with Gofffather's own Sam LaPorta on the Lions' sideline, so this is the one lineup with players on both sides of the Lions' Share. Infinity, last week's high scorers, start Daniel Jones and Terry McLaurin, who play each other in London. Bijan Robinson closes the week, Monday night in New Orleans. Caleb Williams is Doubtful and on the bench, where he belongs this week.

## Trade of the week

Tritium Badgers and Team Simmons each hold the piece the other is missing. Tritium start Kalif Raymond at receiver with Rome Odunze behind him, while Simmons have Michael Wilson on the bench and Kaelon Black in the running back slot that Jonathon Brooks left for injured reserve. The deal: Wilson to Tritium, Alvin Kamara to Simmons, and each of them walks straight into a starting lineup. On ESPN's rest-of-season projections Wilson is the better player, so Simmons' case is the lineup rather than the ledger, and Tritium backfill running back with J.K. Dobbins. Projections lag the news, so both sides should check the week's reports before anybody sends it.

## Before Sunday

Pittsburgh at Cleveland is tonight, and six lineups have a starter in it: the Robots, Gofffather, Team 3, Green Eggs, Simmons and Tritium. Two lineups this morning start a player who is not expected to play, Green Eggs at running back and Win-telligence at running back, and both can be fixed before their games lock. The Lions' Share closes Sunday night in Charlotte, where five of the seven matchups have a starter on the field.
