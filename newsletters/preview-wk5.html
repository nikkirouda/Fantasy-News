Week 5 Preview

Welcome to Thursday. Seven matchups, the first NFL byes of the season (Kansas City and Carolina are off), and a Thursday night in Dallas that six lineups have a starter in. This week the edition keeps the Trainer's Room: for every lineup, the number of starters carrying an injury tag this morning. A bye is not an injury, so Travis Kelce doesn't count. Tuesday we see whose tags turned into actual absences, and the recap names the team that won carrying the most of them. A clean injury report is luck, not skill, so the healthy winners get no prize for it. The projections are ESPN's Week 5 numbers as of Thursday morning and will move by kickoff. Each matchup shows two lines this week: the lineup as it is set right now, and the best legal lineup each team could field from its own roster (one quarterback, two backs, two receivers, a tight end, a flex from those three, a kicker and a defense). Where a free agent would add the most, it gets named. Every free agent named here is available to add now, no waiver claim needed.

This week's matchups

Robots Helping (Themselves) (2-2) at To Infinity and Bijan! (2-2)

  • As set: To Infinity and Bijan! 116.2, Robots Helping (Themselves) 110.1
  • Best lineups: Infinity 118.5 (+2.3), Robots 114.7 (+4.6) · favorite Infinity by 3.8
  • Best free-agent add: Infinity, Aaron Rodgers at quarterback over Daniel Jones, +3.2

The closest line of the week, between two .500 teams, and the Trainer's Room comes down hard on one side. Infinity's starting lineup is clean. Their injuries are all on the bench, including Caleb Williams, who is Out, which is why Daniel Jones is the quarterback. The Robots have two tagged starters, Malik Nabers and Tee Higgins, and both play receiver. Their other choice is at quarterback: Deshaun Watson starts and Jayden Daniels, on the bench, projects higher again. Daniels in for Watson, plus Will Reichard for Eddy Pineiro at kicker, is the Robots' whole 4.6. Infinity's 2.3 comes from Terry McLaurin, Questionable, in at receiver and Juwan Johnson in at tight end. Their bigger gain is on the wire: Aaron Rodgers projects above Jones. Infinity get a head start tonight with Brandon Aubrey kicking in Dallas, and Kyren Williams closes their week Monday night in Los Angeles.

Motorcity-Meh (2-2) at Team 3 (2-2)

  • As set: Team 3 102.1, Motorcity-Meh 82.1
  • Best lineups: Team 3 116.4 (+14.3), Motorcity-Meh 108.9 (+26.8) · favorite Team 3 by 7.5
  • Best free-agent adds: Motorcity, Tyler Allgeier at running back over Tyjae Spears, +2.3, or Jets D/ST over the Eagles, +3.6

Team 3 has the busiest Trainer's Room in the league: four tagged starters (Justin Jefferson, Saquon Barkley, Cam Skattebo and Ladd McConkey) and four more tags on the bench. Barkley is projected for nothing this morning, and the fix takes two moves, because DK Metcalf is a receiver and cannot take a running back's slot. Braelon Allen slides from flex to running back, Metcalf, healthy and at home against Indianapolis, replaces McConkey at receiver, and Isaiah Likely, Questionable, takes the flex. That is 14.3 points, and it makes Team 3 the favorite. Motorcity's injury list is shorter, but they have two empty starting slots. Kenneth Walker is a Chief, and the Chiefs are on bye. Lamar Jackson is Questionable and projected for nothing. Trevor Lawrence, sitting on Motorcity's bench, is one of the week's better quarterback projections. Lawrence for Jackson, Tyjae Spears for Walker and Dallas Goedert for Mark Andrews at tight end add 26.8, the biggest repair job in the league. Spears is the best healthy back on the bench, and Tyler Allgeier, a free agent, projects higher. Even fully repaired, Motorcity trail.

Tritium Badgers (2-2) at Captain Chaos (3-1)

  • As set: Tritium Badgers 109.7, Captain Chaos 96.6
  • Best lineups: Captain Chaos 118.8 (+22.2), Tritium Badgers 111.9 (+2.2) · favorite Captain Chaos by 6.9
  • Best free-agent add: Chaos, Jets D/ST over the Ravens, +4.8

Chaos have the bye week's biggest bill: Travis Kelce at tight end and Chuba Hubbard at running back, a Chief and a Panther, both in the lineup and both off this week. Both holes can be filled from the bench. Kenyon Sadiq takes tight end. For running back, Jeremiyah Love moves over from flex and DJ Moore, Questionable, takes the flex. That is 22.2 points, and it turns Chaos from underdog back into favorite. The one free-agent gain worth making is at defense, where the Jets project well above the Ravens. Chaos also have two tagged starters, Jeremiyah Love and D'Andre Swift, so Puka Nacua and Chris Olave are carrying a lot. Tritium bring two tags of their own, Stefon Diggs and Alvin Kamara. Jonathan Taylor in Pittsburgh and Trey McBride at tight end are why the line is single digits. Tritium's 2.2 is Jalen Hurts for Sam Darnold at quarterback and J.K. Dobbins for Wan'Dale Robinson at flex.

Trade of the week

AMONsters Inc. and Captain Chaos each have a starter the other one needs. AMONsters carry two starting-grade quarterbacks, Joe Burrow and Drake Maye, and start Xavier Hutchinson at receiver. Chaos start Kyler Murray at quarterback and have DJ Moore, Questionable, on the bench behind Puka Nacua and Chris Olave. The deal: Burrow to Chaos, Moore to AMONsters. Both lineups get better for the rest of the season on ESPN's current projections: AMONsters by 4.6 points a week, with Maye at quarterback and Moore in for Hutchinson, and Chaos by 2.3, with Burrow over Murray. Neither team opens a hole this week. AMONsters still have Maye, and Chaos lose only some bench depth, about even against the quarterback upgrade on Sunday. Chaos's spare quarterback, Malik Willis, becomes a free roster spot. Moore's tag and his early bye are what to check before anybody sends it.

Before Sunday

Tampa Bay at Dallas is tonight. Six lineups have a starter in it: the Road Islanders, Team Simmons, AMONsters, Infinity, Gofffather and Win-telligence. Seven starting slots are projected for nothing this morning: Win-telligence and Gofffather at quarterback, Chaos at tight end and running back, Motorcity at running back and quarterback, and Team 3 at running back. Every one of them can be fixed from the team's own bench before its game locks. The biggest repairs: Motorcity 26.8, Chaos 22.2, Gofffather 17.2, Win-telligence 15.3, Team 3 14.3. One defense, the Jets at home against Cleveland, is the best free-agent add for eight of the fourteen teams and worth three points or more to four of them, Chaos, Motorcity, Gofffather and Green Eggs. Only one team can have it; the Falcons and Bengals are the next best. The Trainer's Room count stands at nineteen tagged starters.
