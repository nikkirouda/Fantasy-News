Week 6 Preview

Welcome to Thursday. Four NFL teams are off this week: Detroit, Minnesota, Cincinnati and Miami. That is the heaviest bye week of the season so far, and it reaches twelve of our fourteen rosters. So this week the edition keeps a Departures Board: for every roster, the number of players whose NFL team has the week off, and how many of them are still in the starting lineup this morning, along with the waiver moves each team has made to compensate.

The week opens tonight in Denver, goes to London at 9:30 Sunday morning for Houston at Jacksonville, and closes Monday night with Washington at San Francisco.

As last week, each matchup shows the lineup as it is set right now and the best legal lineup each team could field. Where a free agent would add the most, it gets named. Every free agent named here is available to add now.

First against second, and the Detroit bye has the author covered in departures. Trade of the week

Rickhouse ROYalty and AMONsters Inc. each have what the other needs. AMONsters carry two starting quarterbacks, Drake Maye and Joe Burrow, and at running back they start MarShawn Lloyd and Omarion Hampton next to Derrick Henry. Rickhouse are deep at running back, with Emanuel Wilson one of five backs on the roster, and their quarterback has been a week-to-week choice between C.J. Stroud and Jacoby Brissett. The deal: Burrow to Rickhouse, Wilson to AMONsters. Both lineups get better for the rest of the season on ESPN's current projections: AMONsters by 5.2 points a week, with Wilson in at running back, and Rickhouse by 3.0, with Burrow at quarterback. Wilson's role is the thing to check before anybody sends it.
