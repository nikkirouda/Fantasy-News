# Week 7 Preview

Welcome to the middle of the season. The regular season runs thirteen weeks, so this is the seventh, the one with as many weeks behind it as ahead. Two teams have lost once, two have won once, and everybody else is close enough to the next team to see it. The top ten make the playoffs, the top six skip the wild-card week, and with half a season left very little is settled. This week starts to settle it.

This week the edition changes how it scores the games. Every team is assumed to set its best lineup. Starters back from the bye come back in, players tagged Questionable are assumed to play unless the news says otherwise, and the bench sorts itself out. Byes and lineup holes come up only where a team can't fill one without a pickup. Three teams are in that spot. The lines below are built on ESPN's week-7 projections.

Four NFL teams are off: Buffalo, Jacksonville, the Chargers and Washington. The week opens Thursday night in Chicago with New England, goes to London at 9:30 Sunday morning for Pittsburgh and New Orleans, and closes Monday night with Philadelphia at Dallas.

## The trade desk

The league has made three trades this season, and two of them happened in the last week. One quarterback is in both.

- **Joe Burrow for Emanuel Wilson** (AMONsters Inc. to Rickhouse ROYalty, Wilson the other way). Accepted October 15, processed October 17. Readers may recognize it: it is the exact Trade of the Week from the Week 6 preview, and the first one anybody has actually made. The edition will keep reminding everyone.
- **Jonathan Taylor for Joe Burrow and Bhayshul Tuten** (Tritium Badgers send Taylor; Rickhouse send Burrow and Tuten). Accepted Wednesday morning and now in the league's 48-hour review, which should clear Friday morning. None of the three plays Thursday, so all three play this week for their new teams. The Badgers released Jalen Hurts, whose season is over, to make room. Cincinnati was on bye in Week 6, so Burrow will have played for Rickhouse exactly zero snaps.
- **Kyle Monangai for Michael Wilson** (Road Islanders to Team Simmons, Wilson the other way), from the first week of October. Wilson now starts for the author. Monangai is listed Out this week.

Some of this lines up nicely with the schedule. Burrow's newest team plays his original one, AMONsters. Taylor's first game for Rickhouse is against Motorcity, the second-highest-scoring team in the league. And Tuten, the back the Badgers got for Taylor, plays for Jacksonville, which is on bye.

## This week's matchups

### Robots Helping (Themselves) (4-2) at Team Simmons (3-3)

- Best lineups: Robots 126.2, Team Simmons 114.1 with a kicker · favorite Robots by 12

Simmons have the one roster hole in this game that the roster can't fill. Drew Stevens kicks for Washington, which is off, and Simmons have no other kicker. A free agent like Ryan Fitzgerald of Carolina, or Chad Ryland of Arizona, covers it, and the line above assumes one. The Robots posted last week's high score, and Christian McCaffrey, last week's Player of the Week, leads the projection again. They now get Malik Nabers and Drake London at receiver and Josh Jacobs in the backfield. Simmons have their usual Monday-night ending, Dak Prescott and CeeDee Lamb in Dallas. The running back they traded for, Monangai, is out.

**Stakes.** A Robots win and they're 5-2, nearly sure of the playoffs and three-in-four to skip the wild-card week. A loss drops that to even. For Simmons this is the week the season tips. Win, and a top-six seat is more likely than not. Lose, and they're 3-4 and chasing, with the second-hardest remaining schedule in the league.

### Motorcity-Meh (3-3) at Rickhouse ROYalty (4-2)

- Best lineups: Rickhouse 115.8, Motorcity about 111 with a healthy quarterback · favorite Rickhouse by 5

This is the week's real quarterback problem. Lamar Jackson missed the last two games with an ankle injury, didn't practice Wednesday, and the reporting has him no better than even to play. Trevor Lawrence, Motorcity's other quarterback, plays for Jacksonville, which is on bye. If Jackson can't go, they need a pickup. Tyler Huntley, Jackson's own backup and Baltimore's starter in his absence, is a free agent. So is J.J. McCarthy, who projects a little higher. With either one, this is a five-point game. Without one, it isn't close. Rickhouse depend on Indianapolis. Jonathan Taylor makes his debut, if the review clears, and the Colts' kicker and Josh Downs are on the roster too. Brock Bowers and the Raiders play Sunday afternoon. Motorcity's big two, Kenneth Walker and Jaxon Smith-Njigba, play each other Sunday night, Kansas City at Seattle.

**Stakes.** These two have the most to gain of any pair this week. The winner is nearly nine in ten for a top-six seat and in the race for a top-two seed. The loser falls to about two in three, still alive and suddenly looking over its shoulder. Motorcity have scored more points than everyone but the author, and a 3-4 record would waste them.

### The Provid-End Zones (3-3) at To Infinity and Bijan! (2-4)

- Best lineups: Infinity 127.5, Provid-End 105.5 · favorite Infinity by 22

Infinity are favored by more than three touchdowns, and last week they left more on their bench than any team in the league. They've lost Terry McLaurin, who's Out, but Bijan Robinson and Kyren Williams are again the best pair of backs in the game. Both have a week to make up. Thursday night is the opening round of this matchup: Infinity's Chicago trio of Caleb Williams, Luther Burden and Colston Loveland face Provid-End's Patriots, Rhamondre Stevenson and Hunter Henry, and Provid-End's kicker, Cairo Santos, kicks for the Bears. Provid-End have Amon-Ra St. Brown back from the Detroit bye and Christian Watson at the other receiver spot. Kirk Cousins is now the quarterback, with Josh Allen and Buffalo off.

**Stakes.** Two teams on the playoff line, headed opposite ways. Infinity at 2-4 are close to a must-win: a win keeps their playoff odds around four in five, and a loss leaves them about even and falling. Provid-End are 3-3. A win makes the playoffs very likely; a loss leaves them below .500 and needing help.

### Tritium Badgers (2-4) at AMONsters Inc. (1-5)

- Best lineups: AMONsters 107.0, Badgers 97.1 · favorite AMONsters by 10

The trade of the week is in this game, and it doesn't help the Badgers this week. They gave up Jonathan Taylor, their top back, and the back who came back, Tuten, is on bye. So Sunday's backfield is J.K. Dobbins and Alvin Kamara, Questionable. They do have the week's oddest quarterback decision. Aaron Rodgers and Joe Burrow project within a fraction of a point of each other, and Rodgers plays at 9:30 in London. If the Badgers start Burrow, he faces AMONsters, the team that traded him away ten days earlier. AMONsters got Emanuel Wilson in that deal, and he starts at running back next to Derrick Henry. Drake Maye opens the week for them Thursday night.

**Stakes.** For the Badgers, a loss is close to fatal. A win keeps their playoff odds around two in three; a loss leaves them closer to one in three, at 2-5. AMONsters are already at the edge. A win at 2-5 leaves them a long shot, and a loss at 1-6 leaves them a very long one.

### Artificial Win-telligence (3-3) at Gofffather (2-4)

- Best lineups: Win-telligence 123.1, Gofffather 106.3 · favorite Win-telligence by 17

Win-telligence have four starters tagged Questionable: Jordan Love, Breece Hall, Bucky Irving and T.J. Hockenson. All four are projected to play, and if they do, this lineup is plenty. Ja'Marr Chase and Nico Collins at receiver are as good a pair as anyone's. Gofffather won last week with a man short and are used to winning ugly. Their two Questionable starters, Quinshon Judkins and Emeka Egbuka, decide how close this gets. Bryce Young starts at quarterback again, and Sam LaPorta is back from the Detroit bye at tight end.

**Stakes.** Win-telligence have been even for six weeks. A win makes the playoffs a strong bet and a top-six seat a live one. A loss drops them back toward the line. Gofffather at 2-4 are close to the end: a win keeps them at better than one in three for the playoffs, and a loss leaves them closer to one in seven.

### Captain Chaos (5-1) at Green Eggs and Sam (1-5)

- Best lineups: Captain Chaos 129.8, Green Eggs about 98 with a kicker · favorite Chaos by 32

Kyler Murray is back from Minnesota's bye and back at quarterback for Chaos. Puka Nacua has the biggest projection of any receiver in the league this week. Chaos have the best lineup of the week and the easiest opponent. Green Eggs have a hole the roster can't fill: their kicker, Cam Little, plays for Jacksonville. Fitzgerald or Ryland is the fix, if Simmons hasn't already taken him. And they lost Davante Adams to injured reserve this week, hamstring surgery and six to ten weeks. Matthew Stafford, Jaylen Warren and A.J. Brown, who plays Thursday, are what they have left.

**Stakes.** For Green Eggs, a fifth straight loss is all but the end: their playoff odds would fall to about one in thirty. For Chaos, the stakes are next door. A win keeps them level at the top, and if the author loses in Team 3's building, Chaos are alone in first at 6-1.

### Road Islanders (5-1) at Team 3 (4-2)

- Best lineups: Team 3 126.1, Road Islanders 124.5 · Team 3 by under two

The coin flip of the week, and the one game with first place in play. ESPN gives Team 3 the edge on paper by less than a field goal. That assumes two of their Questionable stars play: Justin Jefferson, back from the Vikings' bye, and Saquon Barkley, back from a hamstring that has cost him two games. Zach Charbonnet is still tagged Out until Seattle decides by Thursday whether to activate him. Braelon Allen projects about the same in that slot. The author has Jahmyr Gibbs back from the bye, the biggest projection of any player in the league this week, and Michael Wilson, the receiver the trade desk sent him, at receiver. Tyler Warren is Questionable at tight end and Darnell Washington starts there. Then there's Monday night. Barkley's Eagles play in Dallas, against the author's Javonte Williams and George Pickens. It's close enough that the last game of the week could decide it.

**Stakes.** A win gives the author the inside track on first place. A loss lets Rickhouse and the Robots draw level with wins of their own, and a Chaos win would put Chaos in first. Team 3 are still the team the model trusts least. A win beats the leader, pulls them within a game of first, and makes a top-six seat likely. A loss puts them back in the crowd.

## Before Sunday

Three teams can't field a full lineup from their own roster. Simmons and Green Eggs each need a kicker, because both of theirs are on bye. Ryan Fitzgerald and Chad Ryland are the best free agents, and they project the same. Motorcity need a quarterback if Lamar Jackson's Friday injury report goes the wrong way, and Huntley, his backup, is sitting on the wire. Everyone else only has to set the lineup they already have. The Badgers–Rickhouse trade clears review Friday morning, so check again on Friday that the right players are in the right lineups.
