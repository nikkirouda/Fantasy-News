# Week 8 Preview

Seven weeks down, six to go. The top ten make the playoffs and the top six skip the wild-card week, and after Sunday there will be five weeks left to sort it out. Three teams sit at 1-6 or 2-5 and need almost everything to go right. Two sit at 6-1 and are playing for the top seed. Everybody else is somewhere in the middle, and this is a week when the middle can move.

Every team is assumed to set its best lineup. A team only gets called out for a hole when it can't fill a starting spot without a pickup. Three teams are in that spot this week, and the lines below assume each makes the obvious add. The numbers are ESPN's week-8 projections as of Thursday morning.

Four NFL teams are off: New Orleans, the Giants, San Francisco and Houston. That bye list takes out a quarterback and a tight end from one roster, a kicker from another, and a defense from a third. The week opens Thursday night with Carolina at Green Bay, and five of this week's seven matchups have someone in that game. It closes Monday night with Chicago at Seattle.

## The trade desk

No trades this week. The last one is still being felt, though. Joe Burrow starts for the Tritium Badgers against Rickhouse ROYalty, the team that held him for a week and never started him. Jonathan Taylor goes the other way, against the Badgers who traded him.

The waiver wire was busier. The Badgers claimed Jakobi Meyers. Rickhouse picked up Malik Willis to cover C.J. Stroud's bye. AMONsters added the Lions defense and Chase McLaughlin.

## This week's matchups

### Motorcity-Meh (3-4) at Robots Helping (Themselves) (5-2)

- Best lineups: Robots 118.8 with a defense, Motorcity 118.7 · even

Robots fans get the first job of the week. The Texans are on bye, the Robots have no other defense, and they need one by kickoff. The Packers are the best one available, and they play Thursday night. The Robots are also without Christian McCaffrey and Malik Nabers, both on bye, which puts Ryan Flournoy in the flex. They've won three straight anyway, behind Jayden Daniels, Drake London and Tee Higgins, and Josh Jacobs plays in that Thursday game too.

Motorcity get Lamar Jackson back. He's expected to play at Buffalo after missing two games, which means the Motorcity lineup needs to change before Sunday. With Jackson, Kenneth Walker and Jaxon Smith-Njigba, this is the best lineup Motorcity have had in weeks, and it projects a tenth of a point behind. Smith-Njigba plays Monday night. That makes him the last word in the closest game of the week.

**Stakes.** Motorcity have scored the second-most points in the league and are 3-4 anyway. A win puts their top-six odds at about seven in ten, a loss at about one in three. For the Robots, a win all but locks a top-six seat. A loss leaves them about three in four.

### To Infinity and Bijan! (3-4) at Artificial Win-telligence (4-3)

- Best lineups: Win-telligence 118.2, Infinity 116.9 · Win-telligence by a point

On Thursday night Infinity's Matthew Golden catches passes from Jordan Love, whose own team is Win-telligence, along with Tucker Kraft and the Green Bay kicker. On Monday night Infinity's Chicago trio of Caleb Williams, Luther Burden and Colston Loveland take the field in Seattle alongside the Bears defense, which also belongs to Win-telligence. Both teams want a Bears win. They disagree about who should get the credit. Everything in between is Bijan Robinson and Kyren Williams against Ja'Marr Chase and Bucky Irving.

Win-telligence need lineup work first. Nico Collins is in the lineup and on bye, and Breece Hall belongs where Pierre Strong is. Chase rested an ankle Wednesday, and Cincinnati called it a rest day. Win-telligence have won two straight. Infinity are coming off a 145-point win.

**Stakes.** Both teams are fighting for the same thing. Each is close to even for a top-six seat with a win and below one in five with a loss. For the playoffs as a whole, the winner is near-certain and the loser is about three in four.

### Team 3 (4-3) at Green Eggs and Sam (1-6)

- Best lineups: Team 3 110.0 with two pickups, Green Eggs 109.3 · Team 3 by under a point

This is where the bye week bites hardest. Brock Purdy and George Kittle are both 49ers, J.J. McCarthy and Isaiah Likely are both Giants, and that is every quarterback and tight end on Team 3's roster. Without help, the lineup projects 83. The fix is a whole Jets offense: Geno Smith at quarterback and Mason Taylor at tight end, both free agents, both playing the Raiders at home. Those two adds turn a 26-point underdog into a slight favorite. Saquon Barkley is the other worry. He was listed as not practicing Wednesday, with the hamstring and a back problem that took him out of Monday's game.

Green Eggs have lost five straight, and on paper this is their best chance in a month. A.J. Brown, Rachaad White and Brenton Strange belong in the lineup ahead of Brian Thomas, Brian Robinson and Dalton Schultz, who's on bye. With those changes Jaylen Warren and Matthew Stafford carry a lineup that runs even with Team 3's.

**Stakes.** For Team 3, a win makes a top-six seat about even, and a loss cuts it to one in four. For Green Eggs, a win is mostly about pride. Their playoff odds rise from about one in a hundred to about one in seventeen.

### Team Simmons (3-4) at The Provid-End Zones (3-4)

- Best lineups: Simmons 123.3, Provid-End 116.1 · Simmons by 7

Team Simmons have gone loss, win, loss, win, loss, win, loss. The pattern says this is a win week, and the projections agree. Dak Prescott, CeeDee Lamb, Zay Flowers and Ashton Jeanty give them the best set lineup in the game, and there's nothing on it to fix.

Provid-End's best lineup has Josh Allen at quarterback, against Baltimore, plus Rashee Rice and Tyler Allgeier from the bench and the Commanders defense. Amon-Ra St. Brown rested an ankle Wednesday, and Allgeier didn't practice. The game has a few crossings. Christian Watson and Tetairoa McMillan are on opposite sides of Thursday night. Simmons's kicker, Jake Bates, kicks for St. Brown's Lions. Simmons's Broncos defense faces Rice in Denver.

**Stakes.** These two are tied at 3-4, and the winner moves to the right side of the playoff line. Simmons go to nearly six in ten for the top six with a win, or below three in ten with a loss. Provid-End at 3-5 would be at about one in ten.

### Rickhouse ROYalty (5-2) at Tritium Badgers (3-4)

- Best lineups: Rickhouse 129.6, Badgers 110.4 · Rickhouse by 19

Burrow against Rickhouse is the headline, but the game is really decided in Jacksonville. Rickhouse have Taylor and the Indianapolis kicker on one side of Colts at Jaguars, and Parker Washington and the Jaguars defense on the other. The Badgers have Bhayshul Tuten and Meyers, both Jaguars. That's six players from one game, and Rickhouse's defense spends the afternoon trying to stop Rickhouse's own running back.

Rickhouse also have James Cook, Brock Bowers, and Willis at quarterback for one week. DeVonta Smith and Tony Pollard belong in the lineup ahead of Omar Cooper and Tre Tucker. Smith missed Wednesday with a hamstring and plays Sunday night, so his status won't be known until late. The Badgers have Trey McBride, the best tight-end projection of the week, and a quarterback with something to prove.

**Stakes.** Rickhouse are playing for the top two. A win keeps that around four in ten, and a loss halves it. The Badgers at 3-4 need this badly. A win puts the playoffs at better than four in five, and a loss drops it to three in five.

### Gofffather (2-5) at Captain Chaos (6-1)

- Best lineups: Captain Chaos 121.4, Gofffather 101.8 with a kicker · Chaos by 20

Gofffather need a kicker. Ka'imi Fairbairn kicks for Houston, so another Jet, Jason Sanders, is the best free agent. That makes three of this week's pickups from the same team, playing at home against the Raiders. Their quarterback, Bryce Young, plays Thursday night, throwing to Jalen Coker and handing off to Chuba Hubbard, who both play for Chaos. Gofffather will be hoping Young has a good night and the yards go to someone else.

Chaos have won four straight. Their lineup needs Coker, Mark Andrews and DJ Moore in for Chris Olave, Devaughn Vele and Travis Kelce. Olave and Vele are both Saints, and New Orleans is off. Puka Nacua is the week's top-projected receiver again.

**Stakes.** Chaos are playing for the top seed, not for the playoffs. Their chance at first is about one in four with a win and less than one in ten with a loss. Gofffather at 2-6 would be down to about one in sixteen to make the playoffs.

### AMONsters Inc. (1-6) at Road Islanders (6-1)

- Best lineups: Road Islanders 129.1, AMONsters 104.6 · Road Islanders by 24

AMONsters did their homework on Wednesday night. They added the Lions defense and McLaughlin, and their lineup has no holes. Drake Maye leads the projection. Derrick Henry's Ravens play in Buffalo, where the author's kicker, Tyler Bass, kicks. The new AMONsters defense is Detroit's, the same team as the author's Jahmyr Gibbs and Jared Goff, and the author's Patriots defense lines up behind Maye. Both teams are on both sides of the field.

The author's lineup is already set, but four starters carry a Questionable tag. Gibbs missed Wednesday with a shoulder on a day Detroit rested several starters. George Pickens, Michael Wilson and Tyler Warren are the other three, and Warren practiced on a limited basis for the first time in two weeks. It's the biggest favorite of the week, as long as Gibbs's Thursday report comes back clean.

**Stakes.** The author is playing for the bye and the top seed. First place is about seven in ten with a win and below half with a loss. AMONsters at 1-6 are one in ten for the playoffs with a win and one in fifty with a loss.
