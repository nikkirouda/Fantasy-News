Week 9 Preview

Eight weeks down, five to go. Two teams are 7-1, one is 6-2, and three are at the bottom needing a small miracle. The other eight are packed between 5-3 and 3-5, and that's where this week will be decided. The top ten make the playoffs and the top six skip the wild-card week.

Same rules as always. Every team is assumed to set its best lineup, and Questionable players play unless the news says otherwise. We only call out a hole when a team can't fill it from its own roster. Two teams have one this week, and the lines below include the obvious pickup. The numbers are ESPN's week-9 projections as of early Thursday morning.

Only two NFL teams are off, Pittsburgh and Tennessee, but they're spread across half the league. Team 3 alone has four Steelers. The week opens tonight with Jacksonville at Baltimore, and the Ravens will be without both of their quarterbacks. Lamar Jackson hurt his thumb in Buffalo, and his coach has said it could end his season. Tyler Huntley is Out too, and reports say an undrafted rookie, Joe Fagnano, will start. Both of those Baltimore quarterbacks are on Motorcity's bench. Cincinnati and Atlanta play at 9:30 Sunday morning, so set your lineups before breakfast. Buffalo at Minnesota closes the week Monday night.

The trade desk

No trades again. That's two quiet weeks running, with the NFL deadline next Tuesday.

The wire was quiet too. Provid-End added Darnell Washington at tight end on Wednesday morning, then swapped him for Chig Okonkwo before lunch. The author swapped Justice Hill for Seth McGowan.

This week's matchups

Road Islanders (7-1) at Rickhouse ROYalty (5-3)

  • Best lineups: Road Islanders 135.4, Rickhouse 124.6 · Road Islanders by 11

The author owns both sides of Detroit at Miami. Jared Goff, Jahmyr Gibbs and Jameson Williams play for the Lions, and Ollie Gordon, in the flex, plays for the Dolphins. Rickhouse have a piece of Miami too: Malik Willis is their quarterback for a second straight week, and Caleb Douglas is one of his receivers. Willis edges C.J. Stroud by half a point, which is as close a call as a lineup gets. Indianapolis is mixed too. Javonte Williams's Cowboys visit a Colts team that includes the author's Tyler Warren, plus Rickhouse's Jonathan Taylor and their kicker.

Rickhouse lost badly last week while Tony Pollard had a big day on their bench. Pollard is a Titan, so this week he's on bye. The author's only lineup question is the second receiver spot. George Pickens and Michael Wilson are both Questionable and project within a point of each other.

Stakes. First place. The author's chance at the top seed: 76% with a win, 50% with a loss. A win also clinches a playoff berth. Rickhouse are playing for a bye. Their chance at the top six: 93% with a win, 74% with a loss.

Captain Chaos (7-1) at To Infinity and Bijan! (3-5)

  • Best lineups: Chaos 127.2, Infinity 125.2 · Chaos by 2

Chaos have won five straight, and their lineup needs nothing. They also have the last word. Kyler Murray and DJ Moore play each other Monday night in Minnesota, both Questionable. Infinity's whole season goes through Sunday night in Chicago. Caleb Williams, Luther Burden and Colston Loveland are home against Tampa Bay, and Infinity's defense this week is Tampa Bay's. D'Andre Swift is in that game too, for Chaos.

Infinity's one fix is Woody Marks into the flex for Keenan Allen. Bijan Robinson plays the Sunday-morning game, against the Bengals defense that Chaos are starting.

Stakes. Chaos's chance at the top seed: 33% with a win, 12% with a loss. A win clinches a playoff berth for them too. Infinity need this badly. Their playoff chance: 89% with a win, 68% with a loss.

Robots Helping (Themselves) (6-2) at The Provid-End Zones (4-4)

  • Best lineups: Robots 138.1, Provid-End 116.4 · Robots by 22

This is the biggest spread of the week, and it depends on a hamstring. The Robots' best lineup brings Christian McCaffrey and Malik Nabers back from bye in place of Blake Corum and Jonah Coleman. McCaffrey didn't practice Wednesday. Leave the lineup as set and this becomes a close game. They've won four straight.

Provid-End need two small changes. Jadarian Price moves ahead of Tyler Allgeier, who beat his projection by more than any other starter last week. And Hunter Henry plays tight end if his foot allows, ahead of the Okonkwo pickup. Green Bay at New England is the crossing. Josh Jacobs is the Robots' Packer and Christian Watson is Provid-End's, while Rhamondre Stevenson and Henry are Patriots, on the other side of the field. Josh Allen plays Monday night against the Robots' kicker.

Stakes. The Robots are playing for a top-two seed: 38% with a win, 18% with a loss. Provid-End's chance at the top six: 62% with a win, 28% with a loss.

Tritium Badgers (4-4) at Motorcity-Meh (3-5)

  • Best lineups: Motorcity 123.3, Badgers 112.2 with two pickups · Motorcity by 11

These teams meet in five NFL games, more than any other pair this week, and the first is tonight. Trevor Lawrence starts for Motorcity at Baltimore. Bhayshul Tuten and Jakobi Meyers play for the Badgers on the same Jacksonville offense. All of them face a Ravens team starting a rookie at quarterback. Motorcity's two Baltimore quarterbacks will be watching.

The Badgers have the week's worst hole. The Steelers defense is on bye, and their kicker is on injured reserve. Harrison Butker and the Giants defense are both free agents. With them, the Badgers close most of the gap. Burrow, coming off the best day in the league, starts the day at 9:30 against Motorcity's Falcons defense. Motorcity lose Pat Freiermuth to the bye, which moves Dallas Goedert to tight end and opens a spot for Mike Evans. Kenneth Walker and Jaxon Smith-Njigba lead it.

Stakes. No game moves anyone's odds more. Motorcity's chance at the top six: 50% with a win, 13% with a loss. The Badgers': 61% with a win, 21% with a loss.

Artificial Win-telligence (5-3) at Team Simmons (3-5)

  • Best lineups: Win-telligence 118.3, Simmons 117.3 · Win-telligence by 1

The closest game of the week, and Win-telligence have the most to fix. As set, they have Jordan Love at quarterback over Patrick Mahomes, Pierre Strong at running back over Breece Hall, and Nico Collins and Deebo Samuel on the bench. Fixing all of that is worth seventeen points. They've won three straight anyway.

Simmons broke their alternating streak by losing twice in a row, so the pattern doesn't tell us anything this week. The lineup question is Kyle Monangai, who's Questionable. If he can't go, it's Samaje Perine. Monangai's Bears host Sunday night against Bucky Irving and the Bears defense, both on Win-telligence. Zay Flowers plays tonight with a rookie throwing to him, after last week's zero.

Stakes. Win-telligence's chance at the top six: 75% with a win, 41% with a loss. Simmons's: 35% with a win, 8% with a loss.

Team 3 (4-4) at Gofffather (2-6)

  • Best lineups: Team 3 122.8 with a kicker, Gofffather 106.2 · Team 3 by 17

Team 3 have DK Metcalf, Rico Dowdle, Germie Bernard and Chris Boswell, and all four are Steelers. Metcalf and Boswell are both in the lineup as set. Moving Cam Skattebo, Denzel Boston and George Kittle in from the bench takes care of the Metcalf spot. The kicker spot needs a pickup, and Andre Szmyt kicks for Cleveland, where Boston and the Team 3 defense play. Last week Team 3 didn't fix a bye hole and lost. This week they're favored even without the fix.

Gofffather have Seahawks for Cowboys at defense and Khalil Shakir to add. Bryce Young and Bo Nix project within two hundredths of a point of each other, so Nix may stay on the bench for a third straight week.

Stakes. Team 3's chance at the top six: 24% with a win, 8% with a loss. Gofffather's playoff chance: 10% with a win, 1% with a loss.

Green Eggs and Sam (2-6) at AMONsters Inc. (1-7)

  • Best lineups: AMONsters 96.2, Green Eggs 89.7 · AMONsters by 6

The two lowest projections of the week, and for one of these teams it will feel like a playoff game. A.J. Brown, now a Patriot, projects zero again and should come out of the Green Eggs lineup. Dalton Schultz takes his spot. Jaylen Warren is a Steeler and on bye. Tonight Brenton Strange of the Jaguars plays against Derrick Henry of the Ravens.

AMONsters have lost four straight, but their lineup is already set and has no holes. Drake Maye leads it again.

Stakes. Playoff chances. Green Eggs: 8% with a win, 1% with a loss. AMONsters: 4% with a win, under 1% with a loss.
