# Week 10 Recap

Deep into November, and nobody has been knocked out. Five teams have now clinched playoff berths, and one of them has a first-round bye in hand. Behind them, seven teams are chasing the last five seats, and for all but one of them the playoff chance is above 65%. A pair of teams snapped long skids this week. And half the league, it turned out, had somebody playing in one football game in Arlington, Texas.

## Results

### Road Islanders 186.1 beat Motorcity-Meh 112.7

The author would like you to know he is being very normal about this. It was the highest score in the history of the league, and it started at breakfast. Detroit kicked off in Munich while most of us were still finding the coffee, and Jahmyr Gibbs had a monster day on the ground and through the air, with a long touchdown run in the fourth quarter to finish it. Emmett Johnson, starting for Kansas City in place of an injured teammate, caught a pair of touchdown passes from Patrick Mahomes. Then George Pickens caught three more from Dak Prescott in Arlington. With the win, the author has clinched a first-round bye.

Motorcity ran into a record, and still played well. Jaxon Smith-Njigba took a pass a long way for a touchdown in Las Vegas, and Trevor Lawrence threw three touchdowns in Nashville. This is still one of the highest-scoring teams in the league, and their remaining schedule is the gentlest anybody has left.

### Artificial Win-telligence 161.4 beat The Provid-End Zones 111.3

Five straight wins now, and a playoff berth clinched. Patrick Mahomes threw five touchdown passes in Atlanta. Ja'Marr Chase piled up well over two hundred yards on Sunday night without ever reaching the end zone, which hardly seems fair. And Deebo Samuel ran seventy yards for a touchdown in, yes, Arlington. Nobody in the league has scored more over the last month.

Provid-End got a beauty from Amon-Ra St. Brown, who caught a pair of touchdowns in Munich, the first a long one that opened Detroit's scoring. Josh Allen ran in a pair of his own in New Jersey. Provid-End are still sitting in the sixth seat, with the bye race wide open behind the leaders.

### Robots Helping (Themselves) 147.9 beat Tritium Badgers 124.7

Christian McCaffrey had the best game any player in this league has had all season. He caught touchdown passes of seventy-four and sixty-six yards, ran in another, and even caught the two-point conversion. All of it in Arlington. Blake Corum added a big day and a touchdown in Arizona, and the Robots have clinched a playoff berth.

The Badgers scored enough to beat most teams this week. Joe Burrow threw for more than four hundred yards on Sunday night. And here is the play of the matchup: on Thursday, the Robots' quarterback, Jayden Daniels, threw the winning touchdown in the final minute to Stefon Diggs, who plays for the Badgers. Both sides got paid on the same pass. The Badgers' closing schedule is manageable, and they are very much in this.

### Team 3 138.4 beat To Infinity and Bijan! 106.9

Team 3 needed one, and got it in style. Brock Purdy threw for nearly four hundred yards in Arlington, including both of the long ones to McCaffrey. On Thursday, Isaiah Likely took a pass a long way for a touchdown and Cam Skattebo ran in a pair. The skid is over, and Team 3 are back to even.

Infinity's bright spot was the Vikings defense, which smothered Green Bay at Lambeau and outscored every other player on the roster. Bijan Robinson found the end zone again in Atlanta. This is still a top-shelf roster, and the simulations like their chances of getting in.

### Rickhouse ROYalty 121.3 beat Green Eggs and Sam 103.5

Rickhouse did most of their damage in one game in Nashville, from both sidelines. Parker Washington went for a hundred fifty yards and a touchdown for Jacksonville, and Tony Pollard caught a long touchdown for Tennessee. Then James Cook broke a seventy-four-yard touchdown run in New Jersey. Back-to-back wins, a playoff berth clinched, and a bye one win away.

Green Eggs had the same Nashville game working for them. Cam Little was perfect on every kick he tried, and Brenton Strange caught a touchdown. Matthew Stafford threw three touchdown passes in Arizona, and the Rams defense barely allowed a thing. That is a team that can still make somebody's December difficult.

### Team Simmons 98.2 beat Captain Chaos 90.1

This one took until Monday night. Simmons led by less than a point going into the last game of the week, and Zay Flowers finished the job in Baltimore. The play that made it possible came on Sunday: CeeDee Lamb's touchdown catch with forty-three seconds left. In Arlington. The skid is over, and Simmons hold the last playoff seat.

Chuba Hubbard carried Chaos as far as one player can. He ran in a pair of touchdowns in New Orleans, the second to put Carolina ahead late, and DJ Moore caught a long touchdown from Josh Allen. Chaos have clinched a playoff berth anyway, and a win this week clinches the bye.

### Gofffather 67.5 beat AMONsters Inc. 60.3

A win is a win, and this makes back-to-back. Chase Brown caught a pair of touchdown passes from Joe Burrow on Sunday night, and Quinshon Judkins scored the winning touchdown for Cleveland in the final minute. Gofffather did all this while starting the Cowboys defense, which was also in Arlington, on the wrong end of it. They are now level on wins with the teams in the last playoff seats.

AMONsters got the oddest stat line of the week from Derrick Henry on Monday night: ten carries, ten yards, two touchdowns. Omarion Hampton had a big night in the same game, and the Lions defense had a fine morning in Munich. They are not out yet.

## For the record

The author's score was the highest single week this league has seen, edging the old mark by less than a point. That old mark was also his, from the playoffs the year before last. His franchise now owns four of the five best weeks in league history; the other belongs to the franchise that is now the Robots. He remains very normal about it.

This was also the best weekend for individual players all season. McCaffrey's game is the best of the season, and Gibbs and Pickens both landed in the season's top four, all on the same Sunday.
