# Week 11 Recap

The regular season is down to its last fortnight. Four teams already have their bye, the Robots have a playoff seat, and the real
story now is the middle of the table, where seven teams are playing for five places.

## Paths to the playoffs

The top ten make the playoffs. Here is each chaser's playoff chance by how its last two games go.

**Win once and you are almost certainly in.** That holds for all four five-win teams. Simmons have the softest landing:
even two losses leaves them a strong favorite.

**The game of the week in the middle: Team 3 against Provid-End.** The winner is all but in, and with a second win,
in the running for the last bye.

**For the four-win teams, one simple rule: two wins and they are all but in.** With one win, the Badgers and Infinity are
about even; Gofffather need both, and play Motorcity and then the Badgers. The Badgers start at Green Eggs, who are out of
the race and free to spoil it.

**Simmons hold the sixth seat**, the last one that skips the wild-card week. Two wins almost always keeps it. Motorcity
are the likeliest team to take it from them.

## The race at the top

**The author:** one more win all but settles the top seed. Only Win-telligence or Rickhouse winning twice and
outscoring the author by about 175 points across the two weeks could undo it.

**The second seed** is the real fight. Win-telligence take it 91% of the time if they win both, starting at Chaos.
Rickhouse are next in line. Chaos have the longest odds and the best road, because they play both Win-telligence and the
Robots: win both and the second seed is theirs 76% of the time. The Robots need two wins, over the author and then
Chaos, to get a look.

**The Robots** clinch a top-six seat with a win this week, and keep it most of the time even with two losses.

## The bargain bin

**Last week:** four teams made a Thursday-evening pickup, and every one paid. Stevens won Monday night, and Justin Herbert
(AMONsters), Justice Hill (Infinity) and Geno Smith (Green Eggs) each led their new team in scoring.

**This week's shelf.** Claims run early Wednesday, and the week starts that same night, Packers at Rams, then the
Thanksgiving games and one more on Friday. All of these are on waivers in our league:

- **Konata Mumpfield, Rams receiver.** Puka Nacua is expected to miss the rest of the regular season and Davante Adams is
  on injured reserve, so Mumpfield is next in line. ESPN's waiver column likes him beyond this week.
- **Kaleb Johnson, Packers back.** Josh Jacobs is unlikely to play (finger), and Johnson led Green Bay in rushing earlier this
  month. ESPN's column calls it relevant volume.
- **Dylan Sampson, Browns back.** The pass-catching half of Cleveland's backfield, and the best-projected back on the wire.
- **Chris Rodriguez Jr., Jaguars back.** Bhayshul Tuten is listed as questionable with an ankle injury.
- **New starting quarterbacks.** Anthony Richardson takes over in Indianapolis after the Colts released Daniel Jones;
  Tommy DeVito starts for New England while Drake Maye's fibula heals; Joe Milton starts for the Eagles at Dallas on
  Thanksgiving.
- **Jason Sanders, Jets kicker.** He has scored every Jets point in a game twice this season.
- **Commanders defense,** at Arizona. The best-projected defense on the wire.

Shop for next week too: Baltimore, Indianapolis, Las Vegas and the Jets are on bye next week, so Richardson and Sanders
are one-week rentals.
