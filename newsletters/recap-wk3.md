# Week 3 Recap

Three weeks in, and the week belonged to the quarterbacks holding clipboards on the sidelines. Most of this league carries a second quarterback, and for most of those teams the backup outscored the starter. One of those backups sat on the bench of a team that lost the closest game of the week. So this edition introduces the Clipboard, an award for the bench quarterback who did the most and got no credit for it. The Anchor stays, same rules: the starter furthest below his projection, verdict at the bottom.

## Results

### To Infinity and Bijan! 161.6 beat Green Eggs and Sam 82.2

A full reversal. Last week's low scorer posted this week's high score, and last week's high scorer was on the wrong end of it. Bijan Robinson did what the team name promises, but the real story is how many of his teammates joined him: all but two Infinity starters beat their projections, and the Vikings defense alone had nineteen points. That happened with Caleb Williams out and Daniel Jones having a quiet day at quarterback. Green Eggs got almost nothing from De'Von Achane or Brian Thomas Jr., and Matthew Stafford outscored Justin Herbert from the bench, a Clipboard nominee but in a game that was already lost.

### Road Islanders 150.1 beat Team Simmons 131.8

Your correspondent's team is the league's last unbeaten, and Jahmyr Gibbs is the reason: Player of the Week, and the biggest overshoot of his projection by any starter in the league. The bench kept Jakobi Meyers' nineteen to itself, which the author will be reminded of. Team Simmons deserve better than this line. They posted the highest losing score of the season so far, with Michael Wilson and Harold Fannin Jr. each more than doubling his forecast. The committee checked: even Simmons' best possible lineup, with Tyler Shough and Zay Flowers in from the bench, finishes about a point short.

### Motorcity-Meh 136.4 beat Artificial Win-telligence 93.0

Jaxon Smith-Njigba again, thirty-five points, with Garrett Wilson having a big day beside him. Motorcity have now scored more points than anybody in the league, and they did it this week with the Eagles defense finishing below zero. Artificial Win-telligence started Nico Collins while he was out, for the second straight week, and their kicker scored a single point. Their backup quarterback, Jordan Love, outscored Patrick Mahomes. Ja'Marr Chase and the Chargers defense kept the score respectable.

### Team 3 124.4 beat AMONsters Inc. 86.8

The 49ers keep paying Team 3. Brock Purdy scored thirty-one and George Kittle more than doubled his projection, which covered a quiet Justin Jefferson. AMONsters picked the right quarterback from a room full of them, starting Joe Burrow over Drake Maye and Baker Mayfield, and lost anyway. Omarion Hampton and the Bengals defense gave them almost nothing, and the bench tight end, Mike Gesicki, outscored the flex starter. They are still winless, and they have the hardest schedule left in the league.

### Robots Helping (Themselves) 121.9 beat Gofffather 71.2

Robots had no Jayden Daniels and started Deshaun Watson, who delivered. Drake London beat his projection by thirteen and Tee Higgins came in from the flex with a big day of his own. Gofffather posted the low score of the week and left Bo Nix on the bench, where he outscored the starter, Bryce Young, by nearly eleven. It would not have saved a blowout, but it leads the Clipboard race going into the verdict, and Gofffather are still winless.

### Rickhouse ROYalty 118.9 beat Captain Chaos 101.7

Brock Bowers nearly doubled his projection and James Cook and Bhayshul Tuten ran for most of the rest. After Week 1 Rickhouse ranked last in every column; they are now a winning team. Captain Chaos started Puka Nacua while he was out, for the second week running, and the bench held Kenyon Sadiq's twenty-three at tight end. Kyler Murray edged Malik Willis by less than half a point, the narrowest quarterback call in the league this week, and one Chaos got right.

### Tritium Badgers 95.4 beat The Provid-End Zones 93.3

The closest game of the week, and both benches could have decided it. The Provid-End Zones lost by about two points with Kirk Cousins on the bench outscoring Josh Allen, and with Jordan Addison sitting behind a quiet Amon-Ra St. Brown. Either change wins the game. Tritium won it while benching Sam Darnold for Jalen Hurts, the biggest quarterback miss in the league, and covered it with Kalif Raymond, who nearly tripled his projection. Jonathan Taylor's quiet day did not matter in the end.
