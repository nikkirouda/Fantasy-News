# Week 4 Recap

This was a week of keys left under the mat. Five of the seven losing teams had a winning lineup sitting on their own bench. So this edition hands out the Spare Key, one week only, to the team whose bench would have won the game by the most. The Anchor stays, same rules. Thursday's edition promised to settle who came out of Charlotte with the Lions' Share, and the answer is Carolina. And last week's question about spare quarterbacks has an answer too: most of the teams whose quarterbacks both played started the right one this time, after most of them did not.

## Results

### Team Simmons 169.0 beat Green Eggs and Sam 106.3

The best score in the league since the opening weekend, and the only perfect lineup of the week: the committee checked every Simmons bench player and none of them would have added a point. Tetairoa McMillan is Player of the Week, with forty-five points for the Panthers on Sunday night, and CeeDee Lamb had forty-one of his own. Green Eggs got a career afternoon from Brian Robinson Jr., far past his projection. They also got the quarterback call right at last: Matthew Stafford over Justin Herbert, by a tenth of a point. It was a good week for Green Eggs, and it ran into Simmons.

### Road Islanders 127.1 beat The Provid-End Zones 89.0

The kitchen-table derby goes to your correspondent, who is still the league's only unbeaten team and would like that noted before the household dinner. The Lions lost in Charlotte, but the Islanders' Detroit wing of Goff, Gibbs and Jameson Williams scored well anyway, and Javonte Williams did the heavy lifting from the flex. His best possible lineup was thirty points better than the one he set, and the defense on his bench outscored the one he started. For The Provid-End Zones, Josh Allen lost to New England, whose winning side included Provid-End's own Rhamondre Stevenson and Hunter Henry. Their Panthers defense finished in the red in a game Carolina won, and for the second week running Kirk Cousins outscored Allen from the bench.

### Captain Chaos 115.1 beat Motorcity-Meh 112.7

The closest game of the week, and the Lions' Share decided it from the wrong sideline. Chuba Hubbard ran for the Panthers on Sunday night and gave Chaos more than ten times the final margin. Puka Nacua, started while ruled out on previous Sundays, was actually on the field this time and led the team. Motorcity got a big day from Kenneth Walker III and a quiet one from Jaxon Smith-Njigba. Their spare key was a single move: Jaylin Noel, on the bench, in for Garrett Wilson wins it. Chaos now sit second in the standings.

### To Infinity and Bijan! 127.1 beat Gofffather 120.8

Kyren Williams nearly tripled his projection, and Infinity needed all of it, because Terry McLaurin was ruled out and started anyway. Gofffather had both sides of Sunday night, Bryce Young at quarterback for Carolina and Sam LaPorta at tight end for Detroit, and got a good night from each. They lost anyway. Khalil Shakir sat on the bench while Courtland Sutton scored a point and change in his place, and that one swap alone wins the game. Gofffather are still looking for a win.

### AMONsters Inc. 128.3 beat Robots Helping (Themselves) 112.2

AMONsters' first win of the season. Joe Burrow and Carnell Tate led it, and Jake Bates, their piece of the Lions' Share, kicked well. Drake Maye on the bench outscored Burrow, the only quarterback miss this week on a winning side. The Robots got big days from Tee Higgins and Malik Nabers. Their spare key took two moves, Keaton Mitchell at running back and Will Reichard at kicker, and the Robots are back to even.

### Artificial Win-telligence 114.3 beat Tritium Badgers 94.7

A fortnight after starting Nico Collins while he was out, Win-telligence started him while he was in, and he led the team. Ja'Marr Chase gave them very little, and T.J. Hockenson's big day stayed on the bench, which did not matter. Tritium got the London game they wanted from Jonathan Taylor, and Alvin Kamara, Thursday's trade-of-the-week piece, led them. They also flexed Dalton Kincaid for under two points with Rome Odunze on the bench, one of the moves that would have won it. Tritium chose Sam Darnold over Jalen Hurts and lost that call by a fifth of a point.

### Rickhouse ROYalty 97.7 beat Team 3 65.9

The low score of the week, and most of it was decided before kickoff. Team 3 started Justin Jefferson while he was ruled out, with DK Metcalf on the bench, and Ladd McConkey and Saquon Barkley added almost nothing. Their bench could have won it, but only with three changes. Rickhouse made the quarterback call Thursday's edition flagged, C.J. Stroud over Marcus Mariota, and it was the right one. Brock Bowers kept rolling. They left more points on the bench than anyone, with Emanuel Wilson and Keon Coleman both having big days, and won by thirty anyway.

**The Spare Key, Week 4 (new this week, retired after it):** Gofffather. Their best lineup, all from players they already had, wins by about fourteen, and Khalil Shakir alone, in for Courtland Sutton, would have done it. It is the cruelest key of the week, because Gofffather are the one team still without a win. Runner-up: Team 3, whose winning lineup was there but needed three moves. The narrowest: Motorcity-Meh, one move from winning the closest game of the week. The author's bench outscored every other bench in the league, and he won anyway, which the committee says is not the same as setting a good lineup.

**The Anchor, Week 4:** Rashee Rice, The Provid-End Zones, zero points against a projection of nearly fifteen, the worst miss by any starter this week. Runner-up: Ja'Marr Chase, Artificial Win-telligence, on a winning side. Third: Parker Washington, Rickhouse ROYalty, also on a winner. Noted and not eligible: Justin Jefferson and Terry McLaurin, both started while ruled out. A player ruled out is projected for nothing, so he cannot miss by anything.

**The Lions' Share, settled:** Carolina won the game 32–26, and Carolina won the share. McMillan's Player of the Week and Hubbard's deciding afternoon both came from the Panthers' sideline. The Islanders' Detroit wing scored well in a loss, and Gofffather, with a good night from a starter on each side, lost anyway.
