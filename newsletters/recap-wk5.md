# Week 5 Recap

Thursday's edition opened the Trainer's Room and promised a verdict today: whose injury tags turned into actual absences, and which team won carrying the most of them. The verdict is below the results, and it is a tidy one, because the league did its homework. Every one of the seven starting slots projected for nothing on Thursday morning was filled by kickoff. The Anchor stays, same rules.

## Results

### Road Islanders 181.2 beat Artificial Win-telligence 107.0

The highest score any team in this league has posted this season, edging Motorcity-Meh's opening-week mark. Jahmyr Gibbs led it and shares Player of the Week with Puka Nacua, and Jared Goff, George Pickens and the Jets defense all came in well over their projections. Your correspondent would like it entered into the record that he also left Jameson Williams on the bench behind Michael Wilson, which the committee has entered. Win-telligence made Thursday's repair, Jordan Love in for the idle Patrick Mahomes, and Bucky Irving doubled his projection on Thursday night. The Bears defense finished below zero, and nothing else on the roster could have helped.

### Rickhouse ROYalty 154.6 beat AMONsters Inc. 94.7

Parker Washington, one of last week's three biggest misses, led Rickhouse this time, and Spencer Shrader kicked for more than twice his projection. Rickhouse started C.J. Stroud over Jacoby Brissett, the call Thursday's edition questioned, and Brissett outscored him from the bench. AMONsters had the bigger quarterback problem: Drake Maye on their bench put up the best quarterback day of any player on a roster in this league, while Joe Burrow started.

### Robots Helping (Themselves) 154.1 beat To Infinity and Bijan! 98.3

The Robots took Thursday's advice at quarterback and it paid at once: Jayden Daniels in for Deshaun Watson, and Daniels outscored him by about twenty. Malik Nabers, tagged on Thursday, more than doubled his projection, and Kyle Pitts and the Texans defense did the same. Infinity’s Bijan Robinson came in short of his projection. Matthew Golden led them.

### Captain Chaos 116.4 beat Tritium Badgers 75.0

Puka Nacua shares Player of the Week, and Jason Myers had the best kicking day in the league. Chaos put DJ Moore into the flex, which was this edition's own suggestion on Thursday, and Moore was ruled out and scored nothing. Chaos won by forty anyway. Tritium posted the low score of the week and left more on their bench than any team in the league, but even their best lineup would have lost.

### Gofffather 108.7 beat Team Simmons 78.0

Gofffather's first win of the season, and they earned it on Thursday swapping Bo Nix in for Bryce Young and Khalil Shakir in for Michael Pittman. Sam LaPorta led them, and the Seattle defense nearly tripled its projection. Simmons had a rough week from the top down. CeeDee Lamb scored under three points on Thursday night, the worst miss by any starter this week, and Harold Fannin Jr. gave them little more. Samaje Perine was the best thing in the lineup.

### The Provid-End Zones 121.5 beat Green Eggs and Sam 106.6

Christian Watson more than doubled his projection, and Josh Allen closed it out on Monday night. Green Eggs got the second-best running back day in the league from Jaylen Warren and the right quarterback call again, Matthew Stafford over Justin Herbert. Brenton Strange sat on the bench, and the Rams defense finished below zero. Green Eggs fall to 1-4.

### Team 3 129.8 beat Motorcity-Meh 125.9

The closest game of the week, and the one where the Trainer's Room mattered most. Team 3 had the most tags in the league on Thursday and made every repair, and Isaiah Likely, Questionable that morning, came off the bench into the flex and led the team. Motorcity fixed their two empty slots. Mike Evans, also tagged, had their best day. One move they did not make decided it: Pat Freiermuth scored more than twenty on the bench while Mark Andrews started at tight end and scored under five. Surprisingly, Motorcity are 2-3 with the second-most points in the league.

## The Trainer's Room, settled

Nineteen starters carried an injury tag on Thursday morning. Seven of them did not play, and every one of the seven was moved to the bench before his game locked. The other twelve played, and the best of them was Malik Nabers.

The team that won carrying the most tags is… Team 3!
