Week 7 Recap

Thursday's edition put up the halfway table: what a win or a loss would do to every team's season. Tuesday reads it back. Five of the seven favorites won, two didn't, and one team's season tipped further than the table said it could. The Before Sunday list gets read back too, and the Anchor stays, same rules.

Results

Road Islanders 126.3 beat Team 3 96.5

The coin flip wasn't one. Thursday had Team 3 ahead by less than two points, and the author won by thirty. Jameson Williams led the way with two touchdowns, his second two-touchdown game in a row. Ollie Gordon scored again from the flex, and Javonte Williams finished it Monday night with the best rushing day of his career. Team 3's two stars at receiver both came in about half their projections, and Isaiah Likely, starting at flex, scored three. Cam Skattebo scored nineteen on the bench. The author is alone in first on points, level with Chaos at 6-1.

Rickhouse ROYalty 136.5 beat Motorcity-Meh 81.4

The blowout of the week, and the trade that paid. Jonathan Taylor's first game for Rickhouse was a good one, and Tony Pollard is Player of the Week with the best day by any player in the league. Rickhouse's two new receivers, Omar Cooper, a Wednesday claim, and Tre Tucker, a free agent, each scored more than twenty. Motorcity took this edition's advice and picked up Tyler Huntley when Lamar Jackson couldn't go, and Huntley gave them a starter's day. But they started Tyjae Spears, who was Out, and played the week a man short. Jaxon Smith-Njigba was brilliant and alone.

Robots Helping (Themselves) 147.0 beat Team Simmons 141.0

The high score of the week for the second week running, and the closest game. Tee Higgins beat his projection by more than anyone on either side, and Christian McCaffrey, Malik Nabers and Drake London all did their part. Simmons scored a hundred and forty-one and lost, the third-best score in the league this week. Zay Flowers, Dak Prescott and CeeDee Lamb all delivered, and Simmons fixed their kicker, as Thursday asked, with Jake Bates. The Robots left Josh Jacobs's twenty points on the bench and won anyway.

To Infinity and Bijan! 145.0 beat The Provid-End Zones 116.0

Infinity were favored by three touchdowns, and they covered it. Thursday night set it up: their Chicago trio of Caleb Williams, Luther Burden and Colston Loveland all beat their projections against Provid-End's Patriots. Bijan Robinson and Kyren Williams were steady, not spectacular. The Buccaneers' defense scored twenty-eight on Infinity's bench, the best defensive day in the league. Provid-End got a good game from Amon-Ra St. Brown, Rhamondre Stevenson and Kirk Cousins, and the Panthers' defense took points away.

Tritium Badgers 113.0 beat AMONsters Inc. 78.3

Thursday said the Badgers had the week's oddest quarterback decision: Aaron Rodgers or Joe Burrow, who was playing the team that traded him. They started Rodgers. Burrow scored more than twice as much on the bench, against his old team. The Badgers won anyway, by thirty-five, with Trey McBride and Makai Lemon. AMONsters were the favorites. Drake Maye had a big day, and Derrick Henry, without his own quarterback, had a small one for the second week running. Darren Waller outscored the receiver who started at flex, from the bench.

Artificial Win-telligence 96.1 beat Gofffather 69.7

Three of Win-telligence's four Questionable starters played. Breece Hall didn't, and Pierre Strong, in his place, scored nothing. It was enough. Bucky Irving led them, and Jordan Mason, back from injured reserve on Saturday, scored seven from their IR slot, where he couldn't count. Nico Collins scored less than three, the worst miss in the league, and they won by twenty-six anyway. Gofffather posted the low score of the week. They started Emeka Egbuka, who was Out, and played a man short for the second week running. This time it didn't work out. Bryce Young had a rough day at quarterback while Bo Nix scored twenty-six on the bench. Quinshon Judkins was the bright spot.

Captain Chaos 117.1 beat Green Eggs and Sam 87.7

Chaos stay level with the author at 6-1. Puka Nacua led them, D'Andre Swift had another good day, and Devaughn Vele beat his projection by more than anyone else on the roster. Green Eggs took Thursday's advice on the kicker: Chad Ryland, one of the two free agents this edition named, scored eleven. KC Concepcion, a pickup this week, scored twenty-three and led them. But they started Brian Thomas Jr., whose Jaguars were on bye, and Brian Robinson, who was Out. Two empty slots in a thirty-point loss.

---

The Anchor, Week 7: Nico Collins, Artificial Win-telligence, under three points on a projection near seventeen, the worst miss by any starter this week, in a win. Runner-up: Bryce Young, for Gofffather, with Bo Nix on the bench. Third: Derrick Henry, for AMONsters, on the podium for the second week in a row.

Before Sunday, read back

Thursday said three teams couldn't field a full lineup from their own roster. All three fixed the hole they were told about. Simmons and Green Eggs added kickers, and Motorcity added Huntley. Three teams then played a man short anyway, for reasons Thursday didn't list: Motorcity, Gofffather and Green Eggs each started someone who was Out or on bye. All three lost.
