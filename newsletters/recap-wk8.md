# Week 8 Recap

Thursday promised three things for today: the read-back on its stakes table, the read-back on Before Sunday, and who used the Jets best. The short answers: three of the six favorites won and the seventh game was a coin flip; none of the three teams told to fill a hole filled it; and the best Jet of the week sat on a bench. The Anchor stays, same rules.

## Results

### Road Islanders 111.8 beat AMONsters Inc. 99.7

The biggest favorite of the week won by twelve, and it took until Monday night to be sure. The author led by more than fifty on Sunday afternoon. Then Drake Maye accounted for three touchdowns in Miami, one of them a forty-eight-yarder to DeMario Douglas early in the fourth quarter, and AMONsters were twelve back with Emanuel Wilson still to play. Seattle brought back Zach Charbonnet and Jadarian Price that night, Wilson didn't record a carry or a catch, and the lead held. Michael Wilson led the author with twenty-seven, on two broken ribs he told reporters about after the game. Jahmyr Gibbs had twenty-five after a slow first half, and Tyler Bass kicked sixteen. The rest of the lineup came in well under its projections, and Emmett Johnson scored twenty-five on the bench. Maye, Mike Gesicki and Douglas carried AMONsters, who drop to 1-7.

### Tritium Badgers 151.1 beat Rickhouse ROYalty 93.2

Thursday said the headline was Joe Burrow against the team that held him for a week and never started him. Burrow scored forty, the best day by any player in the league, and he is Player of the Week. The Badgers had the high score of the week. Bhayshul Tuten scored thirty in Jacksonville, and Trey McBride and the Steelers defense added twenty and sixteen. Thursday also said Rickhouse's defense would spend the afternoon trying to stop Rickhouse's own running back. It did. The Jaguars defense scored twenty-one and Jonathan Taylor, against the team that traded him, scored seven. Brock Bowers was excellent. But Rickhouse started Omar Cooper and Tre Tucker, who scored nothing between them, while Tony Pollard scored twenty-nine on the bench.

### The Provid-End Zones 144.9 beat Team Simmons 88.0

Simmons had alternated losses and wins all season, and this was supposed to be a win week. Provid-End won by fifty-seven instead. Tyler Allgeier, who didn't practice Wednesday and came off the bench into the lineup, scored thirty-six on a projection of eight. That is the biggest beat by any starter this week. Josh Allen, Rashee Rice and Christian Watson all beat their projections too, and Kirk Cousins scored twenty-eight on the bench. Harold Fannin and Dak Prescott were good for Simmons. Zay Flowers wasn't, and that's the next section.

### Green Eggs and Sam 93.7 beat Team 3 67.3

Green Eggs end a five-game losing streak. Jaylen Warren led them, and Jaylen Waddle and Rachaad White did their part. They started A.J. Brown, who was ruled out, and won anyway. Team 3 had the week's hardest bye and didn't fix it. Brock Purdy and Isaiah Likely stayed in the lineup with the 49ers and Giants off. Braelon Allen was the bright spot with twenty-three. Justin Jefferson and Saquon Barkley were steady, DK Metcalf scored one, and Charbonnet scored six in his return Monday night.

### Artificial Win-telligence 134.9 beat To Infinity and Bijan! 126.2

Thursday said both teams wanted a Bears win and disagreed about who should get the credit. The Bears won 34–17 on Monday night. Infinity's Chicago trio of Caleb Williams, Luther Burden and Colston Loveland scored forty-four between them, and Win-telligence's Bears defense scored ten. By then Win-telligence were already ahead. Ja'Marr Chase rested an ankle on Wednesday and scored thirty-one on Sunday. Jordan Love, Bucky Irving, Tucker Kraft and T.J. Hockenson all beat their projections, and Patrick Mahomes scored twenty-five on the bench. Matthew Golden caught passes from Love on Thursday night and led Infinity. Bijan Robinson ran nineteen times for seventy-six yards and scored eight on a projection of twenty.

### Captain Chaos 133.1 beat Gofffather 52.2

The blowout of the week and the low score of the week, in the same game. Thursday said Gofffather would be hoping Bryce Young had a good night and the yards went to someone else. They went to Chuba Hubbard, who plays for Chaos. Hubbard, D'Andre Swift and Puka Nacua scored between twenty-five and twenty-eight each, and Kyler Murray added twenty. Gofffather started three players who couldn't score: a kicker on bye, Kendre Miller with the Saints off, and Emeka Egbuka, who was Out. Bo Nix scored nineteen on their bench, behind Young's ten, for the second week running. Chaos stay level with the author at 7-1.

### Robots Helping (Themselves) 113.8 beat Motorcity-Meh 109.0

The closest game of the week, as Thursday said it would be. Tee Higgins scored thirty-eight, Josh Jacobs scored twenty-three on Thursday night, and Jonah Coleman added twenty from the flex. The Robots won with their Texans defense on bye, Will Reichard scoreless and Drake London at two. Adonai Mitchell, a Jet, scored twenty-seven on their bench. Thursday said Motorcity were getting Lamar Jackson back. They started Trevor Lawrence instead, and Jackson left Buffalo early with a thumb injury that needs surgery. Lawrence scored nineteen. Jaxon Smith-Njigba had the last word on Monday night, as promised, with twenty-four. It was five points short.
