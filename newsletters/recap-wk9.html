Week 9 Recap

Here's the headline: every team in this league is still alive. Nobody has clinched a thing, nobody has been eliminated, and the teams at the bottom still have a path to the playoffs. One of them won this week to prove it. The two leaders both lost, so they're still tied at the top, and underdogs won most of the games. Anybody can beat anybody here, and on Sunday a lot of them did.

Results

Rickhouse ROYalty 149.2 beat Road Islanders 98.9

C.J. Stroud put on the show of the week in Los Angeles. He threw five touchdown passes, including long bombs to Tank Dell and Kayshon Boutte. Rickhouse picked Stroud on a call Thursday rated as close as they come, and he rewarded them with the high score of the week. Brock Bowers chipped in a touchdown in San Francisco, and Spencer Shrader drilled two long field goals in the closing minutes in Indianapolis. Rickhouse look every bit like a team that wants a bye.

The author's Lions ground out a win in Miami with more field goals than fun. His bright spot came in that same Indianapolis game. Shrader's last kick put the Colts ahead, and then Javonte Williams dove in for the winning touchdown with barely a minute left. Good ending for Dallas. A rare happy one for the author on Sunday.

To Infinity and Bijan! 134.5 beat Captain Chaos 108.7

Matthew Golden was unstoppable in Green Bay's rout of the Patriots. He caught every ball thrown his way, ten of them, and scored. Bijan Robinson ran for a hundred yards and a touchdown in Atlanta, and on Sunday night Luther Burden took a pass a long way for a touchdown in Chicago. This is the Infinity roster that rates among the very best in the league, and the wins are starting to come.

Chaos deserve a tip of the cap for getting there at all. They lost their quarterback before the weekend, found a new one Saturday night, and still put up a fight. Puka Nacua was brilliant, catching fourteen passes and a touchdown, and Travis Kelce even ran one in from the goal line. A five-game winning streak ends, but Chaos are still tied for first.

The Provid-End Zones 119.8 beat Robots Helping (Themselves) 105.4

The biggest upset of the week. Christian Watson was everywhere in New England, with seven catches, a long touchdown, and well over a hundred yards. Rhamondre Stevenson caught a touchdown in the same game. Then Provid-End's kicker, Cairo Santos, finished their week in style, with a field goal as time expired to beat Tampa Bay on Sunday night. Two straight wins, and a team playing its best football at the right time.

The Robots got Christian McCaffrey back, and he scored twice in the 49ers' win, once running and once catching. Tee Higgins caught a pair of touchdowns from Joe Burrow. A four-game winning streak ends, but this is still the strongest roster in the league.

Artificial Win-telligence 141.9 beat Team Simmons 121.1

Win-telligence are the hottest team in the league. That's four straight wins, and this was their best score of the season. Ja'Marr Chase opened Cincinnati's scoring with a long touchdown in Atlanta and never slowed down. Bucky Irving ran in two touchdowns, and the Bears defense delivered the play of Sunday night: Jaylon Johnson's interception, returned all the way for a touchdown. After a slow start, Win-telligence have the look of a team nobody wants to see in December.

Nobody in the NFL had more receiving yards this week than CeeDee Lamb. He piled up nearly two hundred yards and scored on a long one just before halftime in Indianapolis. Harold Fannin caught a touchdown in overtime in New Orleans, and Jake Bates kicked four field goals in Miami. Simmons played well enough to beat most teams this week. They just ran into the hottest one.

Motorcity-Meh 105.4 beat Tritium Badgers 104.2

The thriller of the week, decided by about a point. Kenneth Walker had the day of his life against the Jets. He caught two touchdown passes from Patrick Mahomes and ran in a third, and he's Player of the Week. And the Bills defense, picked up on Thursday, held Minnesota to two field goals on Monday night.

The Badgers came within a whisker. Joe Burrow was nearly perfect in Atlanta, missing on only a handful of throws, with three touchdown passes and a fourth on the ground. Rome Odunze caught a touchdown on Sunday night, and the Giants defense, a smart Thursday pickup, had a big day. The Badgers have been climbing for a month, and this one got away by the width of a goalpost.

Gofffather 100.7 beat Team 3 90.0

Bryce Young threw three touchdown passes at home against Denver. Courtland Sutton caught one for the Broncos in the same game, and Ka'imi Fairbairn kicked a long field goal and two more in Los Angeles. It's Gofffather's third win, and maybe the start of something.

Team 3 got a big day from Brock Purdy, who ran for a touchdown and threw for two at home against the Raiders. Isaiah Likely caught the Giants' only touchdown in their win in Philadelphia. It's been a tough few weeks, and the road ahead is one of the hardest left. But this team has the talent to win any of those games.

AMONsters Inc. 89.9 beat Green Eggs and Sam 58.3

AMONsters snapped a four-game losing streak, and their kicker deserves the game ball. Dominic Zvada kicked four field goals for the Giants in Philadelphia. Drake Maye threw three touchdowns, and DeMario Douglas went over a hundred yards. A win, and still alive.

Green Eggs got the play of the afternoon in New Orleans: KC Concepcion took a pass sixty-four yards for a touchdown in the fourth quarter. The Rams defense had a strong day in Washington too. It wasn't Green Eggs' week, but they've shown they can score with anybody.

Nine weeks in: something to feel good about

Road Islanders. The most points in the league, and the best single week of the season, back in week 5. A win over Motorcity on Sunday clinches a playoff berth.

Captain Chaos. Five straight wins before this one, and Puka Nacua is one of the best receivers in football. A win this week clinches a playoff berth for Chaos too. Theirs is one of the toughest roads left, and they control their own story.

Rickhouse ROYalty. Six wins in eight games, and the high score of the week. Their schedule is one of the gentlest left. The bye is right there.

Robots Helping (Themselves). Four straight wins before Sunday, and the strongest roster in the league with McCaffrey back. They still play both of the teams tied for first, so they'll decide a lot.

Artificial Win-telligence. Four in a row after a slow start, and their best score of the season this week. Jordan Love leads their roster in points.

The Provid-End Zones. Two straight wins, including the upset of the week. Josh Allen has passed two hundred points on the season. They're right in the hunt for the last top-six seat.

To Infinity and Bijan! A roster that ranks with the best in the league, and a record that's catching up to it. Bijan has led them all season, and Golden just had a huge day. They lead the chase for that last top-six seat.

Motorcity-Meh. The second-most points in the league, and Jaxon Smith-Njigba has more fantasy points than any player on any roster here. Their schedule is the easiest left, and the record has room to catch up.

Tritium Badgers. Two of the ten best scores of the season. Burrow and Trey McBride are a real one-two, and the schedule ahead is one of the easiest in the league.

Team 3. Brock Purdy is playing some of his best football, and this team was two games over .500 before the skid. Their playoff chance is 70%.

Team Simmons. CeeDee Lamb is over two hundred points for the season, and Simmons owns one of the five best single weeks of the year. Their playoff chance is 51%.

Gofffather. Three wins in their last six games, and Bryce Young is throwing touchdowns. Most of their remaining opponents have losing records.

AMONsters Inc. A win to snap the skid, and Drake Maye has been one of the league's best quarterbacks all year. They're alive, and they'll have a say in who gets in.

Green Eggs and Sam. Still alive. Matthew Stafford and Jaylen Warren have carried them all year, and their last game is against the author, who would very much like them not to.
