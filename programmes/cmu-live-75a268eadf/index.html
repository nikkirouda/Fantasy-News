The series: Central Michigan vs. Miami (OH)

All-time: Miami (OH) leads the series 17-13-1. This is the 32nd meeting in a series that began in 1978 (CMU game notes, Oct. 24, 2026; Winsipedia). Miami has won the last five, all since CMU's 34-27 win in Mount Pleasant on Nov. 15, 2014, and five straight is the longest streak either side has had; CMU's best is three (1978-80) (CMU game notes; Winsipedia). It is Miami's first trip to Mount Pleasant in nine seasons, the last being a 31-14 Miami win on Sept. 23, 2017, and CMU is 5-8-1 at home in the series (CMU game notes, Oct. 24, 2026). The schools also met in two MAC title games in Detroit: CMU won 35-10 in 2007 and Miami won 26-21 in 2019 (Winsipedia; ESPN).

Last meeting: Oct. 26, 2024 at Yager Stadium, Oxford. Miami 46, CMU 7. (The teams did not play in 2025.) Central Michigan led 7-3 late in the first quarter on Myles Bailey's 2-yard run. Then Keyon Mozee broke a 72-yard run to set up Henry Hesson's 9-yard TD pass to Reggie Virgil, and Miami never trailed again (AP recap via ESPN, 2024). Dom Dzioban kicked five field goals, the longest from 51 yards, and Kellan McLaughlin added his first career field goal, a 41-yarder (AP recap via ESPN, 2024). Mozee ran for 120 yards, Brett Gabbert went 10-of-12 for 165 yards with a 36-yard TD to Javon Tracy, and the RedHawks ran for 277 yards on 39 carries (AP recap via ESPN, 2024). Miami held CMU to 168 total yards (106 rushing, 62 passing) and 12 first downs, and in the second half the Chippewas ran only one play in Miami territory, which lost 10 yards on a holding call (AP recap via ESPN, 2024). The crowd was 10,661 (ESPN, 2024).

Game day at Kelly/Shorts Stadium

The stadium

  • Dedicated Nov. 4, 1972 with a 28-21 win over Illinois State. It was called Perry Shorts Stadium until 1983, when it became Kelly/Shorts (Wikipedia)
  • Named for R. Perry Shorts, a Saginaw banker and 1900 graduate, and for Kenneth 'Bill' Kelly, CMU's coach from 1951 to 1966. The field honours former coaches Roy Kramer and Herb Deromedi (Wikipedia)
  • Official capacity is 30,255 on a FieldTurf surface (CMU game notes, Oct. 24, 2026). The record crowd is 35,127, against Michigan State on Sept. 8, 2012 (Wikipedia)
  • The largest on-campus stadium in the MAC. Its original artificial turf was the first in Michigan, and in 2006 it became the last MAC stadium to get permanent lights (Wikipedia)
  • CMU is 191-91-4 here since 1972 and 144-71-4 in MAC games. The Chippewas are 3-1 at home this year and 7-2 at home under Drinkall (CMU game notes, Oct. 24, 2026)
